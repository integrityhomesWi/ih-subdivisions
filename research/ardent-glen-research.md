## A. Expert Summary

Ardent Glen is a new-construction subdivision built by **Veridian Homes** [CL] on Verona's west side, on what was locally known as the "Marty Property" before development [CL]. It sits north of County Highway PD (McKee Road) and west of Shady Oak Lane, near the intersection of County Road PD/McKee Road and Star Light Drive [CL]. The Verona Common Council approved the preliminary/final plat on **December 19, 2022** (4–2 vote) [CL], following a Plan Commission review on **December 5, 2022** [CL]. The approved plan rezoned lots 1–406 to Mixed Residential and outlots 1–25 to Public Institutional, from Rural Agricultural [CL]. Total planned build-out is **258 single-family homes and 148 twin homes (406 units)** [CL]. First move-ins were reported for spring 2024, with phased releases continuing since — a "phase releases" builder blog post dated October 2024 indicates the neighborhood was still building out as of that date; current phase status is **not confirmed for 2026** [CL]. A neighborhood park ("Ardent Glen Park") exists with playground equipment installed per a City of Verona social post; a City planning document also describes a larger planned park in the southeast portion of the site with active/passive recreation and a north-south pedestrian path, subject to Park Board approval — it is unclear whether the completed park matches that full planned scope [CL]. This is a Verona (Dane County) subdivision; no conflicting "Ardent Glen" elsewhere in the Madison area surfaced [CL].
