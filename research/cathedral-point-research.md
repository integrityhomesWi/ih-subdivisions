## A. Expert Summary

Cathedral Point is an established Veridian Homes neighborhood on the southern edge of Verona, WI, first built starting 2009, with **The Woods at Cathedral Point** as a newer, adjacent Veridian phase across Westminster Way — a separate plat, not the same product. `[R1][R2][R3][R4]` all agree on Veridian as primary builder. Original Cathedral Point is largely built out and resale-driven; The Woods is the active new-construction side. `[R2][R3]` name a "Forward Development Group" as master developer with Veridian as builder — not corroborated by `[R1]`/`[R4]`, flag as uncertain rather than stated fact.

## C. Development and Phase History

`[R1-R4], corroborated]` Veridian Homes has built in Cathedral Point since 2009. The original plat and a First Addition were both recorded in 2009. The Woods at Cathedral Point is a distinct, later Veridian phase (preliminary plat ~2019, released October 2020, Section 26 T6N R8E) directly across Westminster Way from the original plat — separate covenants, separate HOA administration per `[R1]`. Some sources (`[R3]`) mention Buell Homes as an additional builder active in The Woods section — not corroborated elsewhere, treat as unconfirmed.

**Not confirmed by any source:** an exact current build-out percentage or whether The Woods has additional unreleased phases.
