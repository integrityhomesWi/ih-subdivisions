## A. Expert Summary

**[PP+CL]** Serenity Estates — full name **Serenity Estates at Token Creek**, also referenced in municipal records as **[CL]** "Token Creek Serenity Estates" — is a newer, upscale subdivision on the north side of Sun Prairie, WI, directly along Token Creek and bordering a ~190-acre conservancy. **[CL, primary-sourced]** Platted in 2021: rezoned to Suburban Residential (SR-4) in June 2021, preliminary plat approved July 2021, final plat approved by City Council October 19, 2021 (Sun Prairie Star/hngnews.com coverage). **[PP+CL]** 39 single-family lots plus 6 outlots on **[CL]** ~35.22 acres, with **[CL]** ~18 acres dedicated to public environmental corridor/wetlands/floodplain/trails — a large public-land giveback relative to lot count. **[PP+CL]** Developer: Coris Development Group, LLC. **[CL adds]** contact Aaron Ruengpinyophun, (608) 615-7750. Marketed in two sections — a premium "Western Plat" (creek-adjacent, $1M+ homes) and an "Eastern Plat" (modern-farmhouse style, ~$650K–$700K) — **[CL flags]** this is a marketing division, not confirmed as a formal platting-phase distinction in city records.

**[CL]** Early marketing copy named Amanda Avenue as a second entry/exit point alongside Lonnie Lane, but municipal plat coverage describes the amended final plat as cul-de-sac-heavy (specifically redesigned to avoid a wetland crossing), and Amanda Avenue is more directly associated with the boundary of the *neighboring* Reserve development. **[PP]** didn't surface Amanda Avenue at all. Don't present Amanda Avenue as a Serenity Estates entrance without confirming against the recorded plat map or city GIS.

## C. Development and Phase History

**[CL, primary-sourced via Sun Prairie Star/hngnews.com]** Full timeline: rezoned to SR-4 (June 2021) → preliminary plat approved (July 2021) → final plat submitted (August 2021) → **action postponed at both the August and September 2021 Plan Commission meetings** while the developer revised the design to avoid disturbing mapped wetlands (an early version proposed extending Lonnie Lane west across wetlands; the amended plat used two cul-de-sacs off Lonnie Lane and one off St. Patrick's Way instead) → Plan Commission recommended approval Oct. 12, 2021 → City Council approved the final plat Oct. 19, 2021, without discussion.

**[CL]** Public infrastructure per the plat: 20-ft water main easement, 20-ft sanitary sewer easement, 6-ft public utility easement, use of an existing 10-ft GTE electric easement, plus a pedestrian pathway and pedestrian bridge across the wetlands between Lonnie Court and St. Patrick's Way.

**[PP]** The covenants document (which both legs independently found) is dated Dec. 23, 2021 — consistent with, and slightly after, CL's Oct. 19, 2021 final plat approval date. No conflict; covenants naturally follow plat approval.

**[PP+CL]** As of Aug. 2026, actively building/selling per multiple current builder and brokerage listings; neither leg could confirm exact current build-out percentage or remaining lot inventory.

**Related but distinct — "The Reserve":** **[CL, detailed]** A separate, much larger project (313 single-family lots, 10 outlots, a dedicated park site, and a site reserved for a future SPASD elementary school on ~201 acres), located north of Stonehaven Drive between Amanda Avenue and School Street — an update to the original "Token Creek Conservancy Estates" Planned Development. **[PP]** mentioned The Reserve only as an unfilled comparison-table row. **Do not conflate the two subdivisions** — same general area, different developments.
