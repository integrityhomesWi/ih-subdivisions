## A. Expert Summary

Eagle Trace is an active, still-developing Veridian Homes community that carries a **Verona mailing address (53593) but sits inside the City of Madison's municipal boundary**, off Old Sauk Road and Schewe Road on the far west side of Madison — near the Town of Middleton line and Pope Farm Conservancy. `[CL]` This administrative split (Verona mail, Madison city services/municipality, Middleton-Cross Plains schools) is the single most important thing to get right about this neighborhood. `[CL]` Construction has been ongoing since 2019 and multiple sources show active phase releases as recently as July 2025, so this is a build-out-in-progress community, not a finished one. `[CL]` **Do not confuse this with "Crest at Eagle Trace,"** a separate, newer Veridian community explicitly branded as being in Middleton, WI, near the same Pope Farm Conservancy area — same builder, adjacent geography, different neighborhood, different price band. `[CL]`
