## A. Expert Summary

Heritage Hills is a Veridian Homes-developed subdivision on the north side of the Village of Waunakee, immediately west of Schumacher Farm Park, straddling North Division Street near its intersection with STH 19/113. Planning began around 2019-2020, the first phase (79 lots — a mix of single-family, standard twin homes, and age-targeted "Haven" twin homes) was approved by the Waunakee Plan Commission in May 2020, and the developer broke ground in late June 2020. As of the most recent information found (September 2025 phase release, "Phase 8"), the subdivision is still actively building out — sources describe a 160-acre footprint expected to build out over 10-12 years from the 2020 groundbreaking, which would put full buildout around 2030-2032. The neighborhood includes a 200-unit market-rate apartment complex (Heritage Hills Apartments, built ~2023, 110 Breunig Blvd) on its southwest corner, plus a mix of single-family and twin homes on individual platted phases. It is served by the Waunakee Community School District, and notably the district's newest Heritage Elementary School (opened fall 2024, 6271 Woodland Dr) and Waunakee Intermediate School (6273 Woodland Dr) sit essentially inside/adjacent to the subdivision itself — a genuinely distinctive, verifiable fact worth highlighting in marketing. An HOA (Heritage Hills Homeowners Association) governs the single-family/twin-home sections, with CC&Rs recorded December 3, 2020 and a first amendment recorded by December 2023, managed by DSI Real Estate Group. Confidence is high on development timeline and school-district facts (multiple corroborating sources); confidence is lower on exact current HOA dues amount, full amenity build-out status, and the complete phase-by-phase street list, which should be verified against primary documents before publishing.

## C. Development and Phase History

- **Original approval / Phase 1:** Waunakee Plan Commission approved a specific implementation plan for Phase 1 in **May 2020**: 79 total homes described as 49 single-family, 24 twin homes, and 6 "Haven" twin homes targeted to senior citizens, plus a pocket park. Phase 1 homes were marketed at the time as "entry-level affordable housing," priced at $308,000 or below, with many in the upper-$200,000s, explicitly aimed at "missing middle income" buyers (2020 dollars — do not use as a current price reference).
- **Groundbreaking:** Late June 2020, following the May 2020 approval.
- **Scale:** Contemporary coverage described the project as a ~160-acre, mixed-use development expected to build out over 10-12 years.
- **Phase 3:** A later amendment to the general implementation plan covered the third phase at the subdivision's northwest corner, abutting Division and Easy streets — originally 35 single-family lots, amended to 38. The Waunakee Plan Commission approved "next two phases" per a dedicated hngnews.com article (exact phase numbers and date not independently confirmed beyond the headline — **flag for verification**).
- **Paving:** A Village of Waunakee news item references "Paving Schedule for Heritage Hills Phase 3," and other reporting references the Division Street extension being graded/paved in 2023 — suggesting Phase 3 construction activity was underway roughly 2022-2023.
- **Apartments:** A 200-unit market-rate apartment complex was approved by the Plan Commission for the subdivision's southwest corner (bounded by Main and Division streets): four 2-to-3-story buildings (37 efficiencies, 88 one-bedroom, 75 two-bedroom units) with underground parking, plus a freestanding two-story clubhouse with business center, meeting/community rooms, exercise/yoga studios, an outdoor roof plaza, and a pool. Reporting at approval time expected the first two buildings, clubhouse, and pool area built first (targeted 2022), with the remaining two buildings following (targeted 2023). The complex is now operating as Heritage Hills Apartments at 110 Breunig Blvd, Waunakee, WI 53597 (a 3-story, 200-unit property per current apartment-listing sites) — architect of record per one portfolio listing: Knothe & Bruce Architects.
- **Later phases (most recent found):** A "Phase 8" release is referenced (Village Board approval of "18 new home lots" per one indexed article headline — full article not independently retrievable due to egress block). A Veridian Homes blog post dated September 2, 2025 ("New Season, New Homesites — September Phase Releases") references a Phase 8 release consisting of 12 single-family homesites (numbered 272-277, 279-284) and 6 "Haven" twin homes (numbered 285-290). Lot numbers in the high 200s by Phase 8 imply roughly 280-290+ total platted lots to date across all phases — this is an inference from lot numbering, not a stated total, and should be verified.
- **Blasting/site conditions:** Village of Waunakee posted official Nextdoor notices about blasting for Heritage Hills Phase 4 construction (dated August 2022), at the corner of North Division Street and STH 19/113, with air-horn warning signals before/after each blast — indicates rocky/ledge terrain requiring blasting during at least Phase 4 grading.

## K. Possible Considerations

- **Active, ongoing construction:** As of the most recent phase data found (Phase 8, September 2025), Heritage Hills is still building out new lots. Buyers should expect construction traffic, equipment, and unfinished/undeveloped adjacent lots for several more years if the original 10-12-year buildout estimate holds (through ~2030-2032).
- **Blasting/rock terrain:** At least one documented blasting operation (Phase 4, August 2022) suggests some lots sit on rocky/ledge terrain requiring blasting during site prep — this can mean higher excavation costs for basements/custom features on affected lots, and was disruptive enough to warrant official village notices.
- **Mixed product density:** The presence of a 200-unit apartment complex within/adjacent to the subdivision's platted boundary (southwest corner) is a meaningfully different density/use than a purely single-family neighborhood — some buyers value the walkability and amenity spillover this creates; others may see it as a drawback relative to an all-single-family competitor community. This should be disclosed plainly rather than downplayed.
- **HOA fee structure varies by product type:** Twin-home buyers pay for a different service bundle (lawn care/snow removal bundled in) than single-family buyers — a buyer comparing "the HOA fee" across product types within the same subdivision needs the fee for their specific product, not a single blanket number.
- **No confirmed subdivision-owned recreation amenity:** Unlike some competitor Waunakee subdivisions (see Section L), no HOA-run pool, clubhouse, or comparable amenity was found for the single-family/twin-home portion of Heritage Hills — its differentiators appear to be school/park proximity and downtown walkability rather than in-neighborhood recreation infrastructure.
- **Future development uncertainty:** With additional phases still being released as of late 2025, the ultimate final footprint, total lot count, and full amenity build-out (e.g., whether the "proposed" dog park and additional parks/trails actually get built) remain open questions. Buyers in early phases bought into a still-evolving master plan.
- **Established-date discrepancy:** Aggregator sites say "established 2021" while contemporaneous news says groundbreaking was mid-2020 — a minor point but worth resolving before publishing a definitive "founded in [year]" claim.

7. Phase 1 (2020) was explicitly marketed as "missing middle" affordable housing (sub-$308K) — a useful historical contrast to show how much the same streets have appreciated since, once real comps come in from MLS.
8. "Haven" twin homes have been part of every phase found (Phase 1 and Phase 8) and are specifically age-targeted — a good explainer topic for buyers unfamiliar with what a "Haven" product line means.
9. The corner of North Division and STH 19/113 — the blasting-notice intersection — is a literal, walkable landmark residents reference; useful for orientation in a "how to find your way around" video.
10. Kwik Trip has three locations in Waunakee (Main St 24-hour store, Water Wheel Dr near Kilkenny Farms, Willow Rd) — worth confirming which is genuinely closest/most-used by Heritage Hills residents specifically (see Part 2 file) rather than assuming the downtown one.
11. The neighborhood's name overlaps with dozens of unrelated "Heritage Hills" developments nationwide (Colorado, Florida, Oklahoma City, etc.) — worth a short disclaimer/awareness note for anyone researching this neighborhood online, since search results get noisy fast (this repo's Part 2 file documents several discards for exactly this reason).
12. Given the original 10-12-year buildout runway from a 2020 start, current buyers moving into new phases today are literally moving into a neighborhood that's still roughly half-built — a good "get in on the ground floor vs. established neighborhood" talking point contrasted against Castle Crest or Westbridge.
