## A. Expert Summary

Crest at Eagle Trace is an **active, currently-selling new-construction neighborhood in Middleton, WI 53562**, built by **Veridian Homes**. It is the "**Crest Collection**" — a distinct, more attainably-priced product line (smaller twin homes / attached homes and simplified single-family plans) sited within or immediately adjacent to Veridian's larger **Eagle Trace** master community. Both communities sit on former farmland a short distance from **Pope Farm Conservancy**, off the Old Sauk Road / Schewe Road corridor on Middleton's far west side. Marketing copy references "three heirloom oaks planted by three Schewe sisters" as a heritage nod on the site. Not confident on exact plat boundaries between "Eagle Trace" and "Crest at Eagle Trace" — see Section D and O.
