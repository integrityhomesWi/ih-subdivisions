## A. Expert Summary

Serenity Estates (full legal/marketing name in most current sources: **"Serenity Estates at Token Creek"**, also referenced in municipal records as **"Token Creek Serenity Estates"**) is a newer, upscale subdivision on the north side of Sun Prairie, WI, platted in 2021 and building out through the mid-2020s. It sits directly along Token Creek and borders a 190-acre conservancy, giving it a wooded/waterfront feel unusual for Sun Prairie new construction. The plat was approved for 39 single-family residential lots and six outlots on roughly 35.22 acres, with nearly 18 acres dedicated to public environmental corridor, wetlands, floodplain, trails, and open space — a large public-land giveback relative to lot count. The development is split by local marketing into a "Western Plat" (premium, larger, creek-adjacent lots, homes marketed at $1M+) and an "Eastern Plat" (modern-farmhouse-style homes marketed around $650,000–$700,000, as of the source dates found — treat as builder-quoted, not sold data). The developer is Coris Development Group, LLC (McFarland/Sun Prairie, WI); builders working in the community include William Ryan Homes and at least one custom builder, Victory Homes of Wisconsin. This is a small, low-lot-count, high-end infill/edge-of-city subdivision — not a mass-production neighborhood — which should shape how it's marketed relative to larger nearby developments like The Reserve.

**Confidence flags:** HOA dues amount, exact current build-out percentage, and definitive school assignment for every section were NOT confirmed from public sources in this pass — see Section O.

## C. Development and Phase History

- **Original land use:** Rural/agricultural land along Token Creek prior to annexation and platting; no evidence found of prior residential use on the parcel.
- **Developer:** Coris Development Group, LLC, based in the Sun Prairie/McFarland area, is named as developer/applicant in city rezoning and plat materials.
- **Zoning:** Rezoned to Suburban Residential (SR-4) by Sun Prairie City Council, approved June 2021.
- **Plat process:**
  - Plan Commission recommended approval of preliminary and final plat Oct. 12, 2021 (per Sun Prairie Star / hngnews.com coverage).
  - Council approved the **preliminary plat** in **July 2021**.
  - Developer submitted the **final plat** in **August 2021**; action was **postponed at both August and September 2021 Plan Commission meetings** while the developer revised the design to avoid disturbing mapped wetlands — specifically, an early version proposed extending Lonnie Lane west across wetlands; the amended plat instead used two cul-de-sacs extending east and north from Lonnie Lane and a cul-de-sac north from St. Patrick's Way, avoiding the wetland crossing.
  - City Council approved the final plat **Oct. 19, 2021**, without discussion, per hngnews.com.
- **Phasing/marketing split:** Local builder/marketing materials (Coris/serenityestateshomes.com) describe a **"Western Plat"** (premium/creek-adjacent, $1M+ homes) and an **"Eastern Plat"** (modern farmhouse style, ~$650K–$700K). This is a marketing division, not necessarily a formal platting-phase distinction confirmed in city records — flag for confirmation.
- **Public infrastructure:** Plat included a 20-ft water main easement, 20-ft sanitary sewer easement, 6-ft public utility easement, and use of an existing 10-ft GTE electric easement, plus a pedestrian pathway and pedestrian bridge across the wetlands between Lonnie Court and St. Patrick's Way.
- **Status as of this research (Aug. 2026):** Actively building/selling per multiple active builder and brokerage listing pages (William Ryan Homes community page, Stark Company, exp Realty, Lauer Realty Group, etc.). Not confirmed whether the plat is fully built out — flag for John to verify current lot inventory.
- **Related nearby project (do not conflate):** "The Reserve" is a separate, much larger project (313 single-family lots, 10 outlots, a park site, and a site reserved for a future Sun Prairie Area School District elementary school on ~201 acres) located north of Stonehaven Drive between Amanda Avenue and School Street — described as an update to the original "Token Creek Conservancy Estates" Planned Development. This is a different subdivision from Serenity Estates, though in the same Token Creek-area corridor.

## K. Possible Considerations

- **Small, still-developing plat:** With only 39 lots, Serenity Estates is a small subdivision by Sun Prairie new-construction standards; buyers should understand this is not a large master-planned community with extensive shared amenities (compare to The Reserve's 313 lots, which does include a park site and a planned elementary school site).
- **Wetland/floodplain proximity:** The plat's own approval process involved redesigning the road layout specifically to avoid disturbing wetlands, and nearly half the platted acreage is dedicated to environmental corridor/wetlands/floodplain. Buyers on wetland-adjacent lots should understand any applicable buffer/building setback rules and flood-plain considerations — verify per-lot with the city or a survey.
- **Two-tier pricing/design within one plat:** The Western vs. Eastern Plat naming and differing minimum square footages/price points mean "Serenity Estates" is not architecturally or price-uniform — a buyer's experience differs meaningfully depending on which side of the subdivision they're in. Marketing copy should be specific about which plat/section is being described.
- **HOA restriction uncertainty:** Because the full covenants document could not be directly verified in this pass, don't assume typical suburban HOA allowances (e.g., on sheds, fences, rentals, backyard structures) — confirm before advising a buyer, especially anyone planning short-term rental use, extensive landscaping, or accessory structures.
- **Limited egress:** The amended plat's cul-de-sac-heavy layout (rather than a through-street to the west) may mean fewer routes in/out of parts of the subdivision — worth confirming for buyers concerned about emergency-vehicle access or traffic in growing sections.
- **New development, limited resale history:** Because this plat was only approved in 2021 and is still an active-build community, resale/comp history is thin — flag for buyers expecting deep comparable-sales data, and rely on the SCWMLS export for actual current pricing rather than any figure in this document.
