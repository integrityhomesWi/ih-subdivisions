C.  Development and Phase History  
Verified facts:  
- The original land planning documents (circa May 2020) show Heritage Hills at the eastern gateway of Waunakee along Highway 19 between Schumacher Road and Division Street, partially annexed and formerly agricultural ([waunakee.gov](https://www.waunakee.gov/Archive/ViewFile/Item/1144?utm_source=openai)).  
- Veridian announced the first release of 49 home sites in November 2020, including single-family, twin, and Haven product lines, with mention of 20 acres of parks ([veridianhomes.com](https://veridianhomes.com/blog/2020/11/18/welcome-home-to-heritage-hills/?utm_source=openai)).  
- Phase 6 final plat was approved/reviewed March 11, 2024 ([ob.waunakee.com](https://ob.waunakee.com/OnBaseAgendaOnline/Documents/DownloadFileBytes/Plan_Commission_2228_Agenda_3_11_2024_5_30_00_PM.pdf?documentType=1&meetingId=2228&utm_source=openai)).  
- Phase 8 (called 7th Addition) comprising 13 single-family lots and 6 twin-home units was under Plan Commission consideration as of April 14, 2025 ([ob.waunakee.com](https://ob.waunakee.com/OnBaseAgendaOnline/Documents/DownloadFileBytes/Plan_Commission_2241_Agenda_Packet_4_14_2025_5_30_00_PM.pdf?documentType=5&isAttachment=True&meetingId=2241&utm_source=openai)).  
- The Heritage Hills Apartments (multi-family apartments) completed in 2023—200 units across two 3-story buildings with clubhouse and amenities ([knothebruce.com](https://www.knothebruce.com/projects/heritage-hills?utm_source=openai)).  

Reasonable inferences: development began roughly late 2020/early 2021, in phases; more phases likely pending beyond Phase 8.
