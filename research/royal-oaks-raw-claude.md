## A. Expert Summary

Royal Oaks is an established, mature single-family residential neighborhood
on the west-central side of Sun Prairie, WI, built out mostly in the late
1960s–early 1970s (with some infill from later decades). It reads as a
classic first-ring "starter/move-up" Sun Prairie neighborhood: ranch,
bi-level, and Colonial Revival homes on larger lots (up to roughly a third
of an acre) with mature tree canopy, four neighborhood parks, and both an
elementary and a middle school inside or immediately adjacent to its
boundaries. It is **not confident this is a platted "subdivision" in the
formal HOA-governed sense** — sources describe it as an informal, long-established
neighborhood/area name rather than a developer-branded subdivision with a
recorded HOA. This should be verified against Dane County plat records
before being described to a client as having (or lacking) HOA governance.

## C. Development and Phase History

No plat records, developer name, or phase/section history were found in this
research pass. Aggregator sites consistently describe home construction as
concentrated in the late 1960s to early 1970s, with some later infill homes,
but none cite a plat map, developer, or phased build-out. **This is a gap —
recommend pulling the Dane County plat/parcel record or City of Sun Prairie
planning archive to confirm actual platting date(s) and any phases.** Unlike
newer subdivisions (e.g., Kilkenny Farms West), Royal Oaks reads as long
since fully built out — there is no indication of active new construction,
open lots, or a builder currently selling in the neighborhood.

## D. Location, Streets, and Boundaries

One source (real-estate aggregator) gives explicit boundary streets:

- **North:** Windsor Street
- **South:** Colorado Avenue
- **East:** Broadway Drive
- **West:** Thompson Road

A second, independent description (homes.com-derived summary) places Royal
Oaks "north-east of Wyndham Hills and south-west of Vandenburg," which is
directionally consistent with a west-central Sun Prairie location. These two
descriptions do not contradict each other, but the boundary streets come from
a single aggregator source and were **not independently verified against a
city GIS or plat map in this pass** — treat as a strong lead, not a confirmed
legal boundary, especially at the edges.

## E. Schools

Sun Prairie Area School District (SPASD). Per SPASD's own secondary boundary
documentation (2021 boundary-change process), the area "north of Highway 19,
west of Broadway Drive, and east of Thompson Road" is in the **Prairie View
Middle School** attendance area — this matches Royal Oaks' east/west boundary
streets exactly, which is a good corroborating signal.

- **Elementary:** Royal Oaks Elementary, 2215 Pennsylvania Ave — named after
  and physically located in the neighborhood; described as the only
  open-concept school in SPASD. Reasonable to assume in-boundary, but SPASD
  elementary boundaries are not universally coterminous with neighborhood
  names — **verify by address.**
- **Middle school:** Prairie View Middle School, 400 N. Thompson Road —
  boundary description corroborates Royal Oaks' street boundaries. **Verify
  by address** before stating as guaranteed for any specific listing.
- **High school:** SPASD split into **Sun Prairie East** (888 Grove Street)
  and **Sun Prairie West** (2850 Ironwood Drive) in 2022. One source
  describes the post-split dividing line as roughly Highways N and 151, with
  the central city area west of that line assigned to Sun Prairie West.
  Royal Oaks' exact assignment (East vs. West) was **not confirmed** in this
  pass — **do not state a high school assignment for this neighborhood
  without checking a specific address against SPASD's current boundary tool
  or map (sunprairieschools.org).**

## K. Possible Considerations

- Home ages (1960s–70s construction) mean typical resale-home
  considerations: original mechanicals/roofs likely replaced multiple times
  over, potential for knob-and-tube or older electrical in un-updated homes,
  possible lack of modern insulation standards — **not confirmed
  neighborhood-wide**, varies home to home, but worth flagging as a general
  "older neighborhood" consideration rather than a specific defect claim.
- No confirmed HOA means no confirmed shared-amenity funding mechanism or
  architectural control — could be a pro (no HOA fee/rules) or a con
  (no enforced upkeep standard) depending on buyer preference; frame neutrally.
- High school assignment uncertainty (East vs. West, post-2022 split) is a

- Aggregator-cited median price and population figures are unverified and

## M. Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Royal Oaks a new subdivision?**
No — it's an established, mostly built-out neighborhood dating primarily to
the late 1960s–early 1970s, with some later infill. There is no evidence of
active new-home construction in Royal Oaks itself (unlike Kilkenny Farms
West).

**Does Royal Oaks have an HOA?**
Not confirmed either way in this research pass. No HOA name or fee was
found; recommend verifying via title work or a current listing agent before
telling a buyer there is or isn't one.

**What schools serve Royal Oaks?**
Royal Oaks Elementary (2215 Pennsylvania Ave) and Prairie View Middle School
(400 N. Thompson Road) are both named for/located near the neighborhood and
the middle-school boundary description corroborates Royal Oaks' street
boundaries. High school assignment (Sun Prairie East vs. West) is **not
confirmed** — verify by address.

**What parks are in Royal Oaks?**
Royal Oaks Park, Evergreen Park, Oakridge Park, and Oakview Park are
consistently cited as neighborhood parks.

**How far is Royal Oaks from downtown Madison?**
One source cites roughly 12 miles via Highway 151 to downtown Madison;
treat as an approximate, unverified figure pending a direct routing check
(see companion conveniences file).
