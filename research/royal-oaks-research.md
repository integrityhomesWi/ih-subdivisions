## A. Expert Summary

Royal Oaks is an established, mature single-family residential neighborhood
on the west-central side of Sun Prairie, WI, built out mostly in the late
1960s–early 1970s (with some infill from later decades). **[CL]** It reads as
a classic first-ring "starter/move-up" Sun Prairie neighborhood: ranch,
bi-level, and Colonial Revival homes on larger lots (up to roughly a third of
an acre) with mature tree canopy, four neighborhood parks, and both an
elementary and a middle school inside or immediately adjacent to its
boundaries. **[CL]** It is **not confident this is a platted "subdivision" in
the formal HOA-governed sense** — sources describe it as an informal,
long-established neighborhood/area name rather than a developer-branded
subdivision with a recorded HOA. **[CL]** This should be verified against Dane
County plat records before being described to a client as having (or lacking)
HOA governance.
