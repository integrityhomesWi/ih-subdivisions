## ⚠️ CRITICAL NAME/LOCATION FLAG — READ FIRST

The brief was requested for "Village at Autumn Lake, Sun Prairie, Wisconsin." Every source found is **consistent and unanimous** on one point that conflicts with that framing:

**Village at Autumn Lake has a City of Madison address (ZIP 53718), not a Sun Prairie address.** It sits on the far east side of the City of Madison, bordered by I-39/90 to the west, Felland Road to the east, and Lien Road to the south — but it is zoned into the **Sun Prairie Area School District**, not the Madison Metropolitan School District. This "Madison address / Sun Prairie schools" split is repeated verbatim across the developer (Veridian Homes), multiple brokerage sites, and rental listing sites — it is not a one-off error.

This is exactly the "do not conflate" trap the project brief warns about, just inverted: instead of two different subdivisions sharing a similar name, this is one subdivision whose municipal address (Madison) does not match the city label commonly used in marketing/schools context (Sun Prairie). **Do not publish content that states or implies the property address is "Sun Prairie, WI"** — the mailing address is Madison, WI 53718. The Sun Prairie connection is real but is a *school-district* fact, not a *city-of-record* fact. Flag this to John explicitly; he may want the piece framed as "Sun Prairie schools, Madison address" rather than "Sun Prairie subdivision."

A separate, genuinely-Sun-Prairie Veridian Homes community exists nearby and should **not** be confused with this one: **The Reserve**, Sun Prairie, WI 53590, on Sun Prairie's west side — a different plat, different address, different price point (starting ~$598K per one source vs. Village at Autumn Lake's ~$419-440K starting price). See Section L.

## A. Expert Summary

Village at Autumn Lake is a Veridian Homes single-family/townhome community on the far east side of the City of Madison, Dane County, built around a man-made lake (sources disagree on size — "15-acre" and "16-acre" both appear; flagged as a conflict, not resolved). It carries a Madison, WI 53718 mailing address but is zoned into the Sun Prairie Area School District, a split repeated consistently across developer and third-party sources. The neighborhood is bounded roughly by I-39/90 to the west, Felland Road to the east, and Lien Road to the south. It is an active, still-building Veridian Homes project — the developer's own June 2026 blog post announced new homesites released in "The Reserve & Village at Autumn Lake," confirming ongoing construction as of mid-2026. Current listed floor plans (per aggregator sites, dated to this research pass) span roughly 1,482–3,044 sq ft, 3–4 bed / 2–4 bath, with community-wide pricing described as starting in the $419,900–$439,900 range depending on source and date — treat these as builder list prices for new construction, not resale comps, and date-stamp them 2026 when used. Planned/built amenities include walking trails (a "7 miles of trails" figure appears in marketing copy), open park space, a dog park, a community garden, a playground, and a sledding hill; a Sun Prairie elementary school site was planned along the neighborhood's eastern edge per one Veridian planning-map PDF, but no source confirms that school has actually opened — treat as a planned facility, not a completed one, unless separately verified. A distinct sub-section called "Woods at Autumn Lake" is marketed as the newest, more premium enclave within/adjacent to the larger Village at Autumn Lake footprint, with larger homesites and a more wooded character — worth distinguishing in any content that discusses lot size or price tier. The HOA is managed by DSI Real Estate Group; reported annual dues vary by source/property/year from roughly $216 to $325, which likely reflects different lot types (single-family vs. townhome) and/or different years rather than one uniform fee — do not present a single dues figure as the community-wide number. Confidence is high on the Madison-address/Sun Prairie-schools split, the developer, and the general boundary streets; confidence is medium-to-low on exact current HOA dues, exact current price ranges, and whether the planned elementary school and full amenity build-out are complete — all flagged below for John to verify directly.
