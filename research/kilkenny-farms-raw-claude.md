## A. Expert Summary

Kilkenny Farms is an established, largely built-out residential neighborhood on the south side of Waunakee, WI, originating around 2014. It sits east of County Road Q and south of Woodland Drive, and is anchored by a mixed commercial node along Water Wheel Drive (Kwik Trip, Buck & Honey's, a former Animart store, and other neighborhood-serving businesses) plus Kilkenny Farms Park — a 5.42-acre village park with a shelter, playground, tennis/basketball/volleyball courts, and six regulation pickleball courts — and a private/HOA-affiliated neighborhood pool. It is NOT the same development as **Kilkenny Farms West**, a newer 28-acre subdivision (Village TID 6 expansion approved by the Waunakee Village Board in 2024–2025, developer associated with Don Tierney/Livable Communities) that sits nearby, still under active construction as of 2026, and is anchored by its own forthcoming Aldi and a separate commercial node "directly across Highway Q." Several real-estate marketing sites blend the two names, so every fact below has been checked against sources that specifically say "Kilkenny Farms" (not "West") where possible; ambiguous items are flagged.

Notably, at least one national new-home-search aggregator (newhomesource.com) lists "Kilkenny Farms" as an **archived** community listing — consistent with it being a largely sold-out, built-out neighborhood rather than one with active lot inventory, in contrast to Kilkenny Farms West's active marketing as a lot-sale community.

## C. Development and Phase History

- **Origin:** Multiple independent sources (madcitydreamhomes.com summary, neighborhoods.com aggregation) converge on "Kilkenny Farms originated in 2014, with new construction still popping up" as of source publication (undated, likely 2023–2025 content) — treat the 2014 start year as reasonably reliable but not primary-source-verified (no plat/CSM document was located in this pass).
- **Builder(s):** Classic Custom Homes of Waunakee, Inc. lists Kilkenny Farms among its subdivisions (cchofwaunakee.com/kilkenny-farms-subdivision/). No other specific production or custom builder was confirmed as active in Kilkenny Farms proper in this research pass (search results kept surfacing Kilkenny Farms **West** builder info instead — e.g., Espire Home Builders, Victory Homes — which is out of scope here).
- **Build-out status:** newhomesource.com carries a listing titled "Kilkenny Farms-archived in Waunakee, WI," which strongly suggests the community is now considered built out / no longer actively selling new lots, consistent with a 2014-vintage neighborhood in 2026. This is a reasonable inference, not a confirmed statement from a planning document.
- **Adjacent/successor development:** A **separate** 28-acre "Kilkenny West" (referred to in Village Board coverage as "Kilkenny West," and marketed by realtors as "Kilkenny Farms West") was the subject of a Village of Waunakee TID (Tax Incremental District) 6 project-plan amendment. hngnews.com reported the Village Board approved the TID 6 expansion to include this 28-acre development on a 4–3 vote (dissenting trustees Nila Frye, Sam Kaufmann, Robert McPherson); the Village agreed to reimburse the developer ("Tierney," i.e., Don Tierney / Livable Communities) $2.8M for public infrastructure, with the developer committing $5.04M toward infrastructure including a Woodland Drive path. Plans for this newer development are "mostly zoned residential" with "several mixed-use and commercial lots." This is Kilkenny Farms West territory, not Kilkenny Farms, but establishes the direct adjacency and shared-name risk. **Flag:** exact TID vote date conflicts slightly between sources found (one references April 2024 draft, another an April 15, 2025 board vote) — do not state a single date without further verification.
- **Commercial node:** A Village of Waunakee document titled "Kilkenny-Farms-Commercial" (waunakee.gov/DocumentCenter, PDF) confirms an official planning designation of a commercial component tied to the Kilkenny Farms name along Water Wheel Drive — this is the Kwik Trip/Buck & Honey's/former Animart corridor. Other uses referenced near this corridor in search results include Waunakee Community Bank, First Choice Dental, Rainbow Daycare, a boutique-style hotel, multi-family housing, and Home Again senior living — these appear associated with the broader Water Wheel Drive commercial park rather than confirmed as literally inside the Kilkenny Farms residential plat; treat as "in the immediate vicinity" rather than "inside the subdivision" pending confirmation.
