## Discarded Due to Name Collision

- **No businesses or places named "University Hill Farms" outside of Madison, WI were returned in any search query in this research pass** — the search results consistently and exclusively referenced the Madison, WI near-west-side neighborhood (hillfarms.org, the National Register historic district, madison.com local news, MMSD-adjacent school listings). Nothing required discarding on a same-name-different-place basis for the core subdivision name itself.
- **Discarded: Sequoyah High School (Tennessee)** and **Sequoyah High School (Claremore, Oklahoma)** — surfaced in a search for "Sequoya Library" due to name similarity ("Sequoya"/"Sequoyah"); these are unrelated Tennessee/Oklahoma high schools, not connected to Madison's Sequoya Library branch. Discarded as irrelevant name-collision noise, not used anywhere in this brief.
- **Note on "Hill Farms" ambiguity (not a discard, but a flag):** "Hill Farms" is used both as (a) the informal short name for the University Hill Farms residential neighborhood, and (b) part of the formal name of the unrelated-but-adjacent "Hill Farms State Office Building" / "Madison Yards at Hill Farms" state government/mixed-use redevelopment project. Both are real, both are in the same physical vicinity, but they are institutionally distinct (one is a residential neighborhood with a voluntary association; the other is a State of Wisconsin capital project with private mixed-use development partners). This brief treats them as distinct throughout and flags this distinction in Section A/H of the raw file — not a collision to discard, but a conflation risk worth calling out explicitly given the project's stated concern about mixing up similarly-named things.
- **Note on pricing sources (not a name collision, but a discard-by-rule):** Zillow/Redfin/Trulia-sourced average-sale-price and days-on-market figures surfaced in search results (e.g., an aggregator citing "$577,033 average sale price" and "42 days on market") were **deliberately discarded/excluded** from both files per the research rules — these are marked "pending MLS data from John" rather than reported as fact.
