## A. Expert Summary

Bear Tree Farms is a large, multi-phase, still-actively-developing single-family (plus some attached/condo) subdivision straddling the DeForest/Windsor/Sun Prairie area of Dane County, WI. **Important nuance for John:** despite being marketed heavily as "DeForest, WI" by builders and portals (probably because it uses a DeForest, WI 53532 mailing address), the subdivision is physically located in, and governed/platted by, the **Village of Windsor** — not the Village/Town limits of DeForest proper. It sits near the boundary between Windsor and Sun Prairie, off Windsor Road and Pederson Crossing Boulevard, with access to US Hwy 51. Schools are DeForest Area School District (Windsor Elementary, DeForest Middle, DeForest High), which is the connective tissue that makes the "DeForest" branding reasonable even though the municipality is Windsor. Development began around 2018 and has proceeded in at least 8 phases through 2025, built out by multiple builders (Tim O'Brien Homes, Eldon Homes, and custom builders via Design Custom Homes of Madison). The neighborhood includes a large (~22-acre) community park with splash pad, sport courts, and trails, plus a small commercial node (Door Creek Church campus, Paradise Paws Doggie Day Care, Yahara River Learning Center, Cortland Park condos). A 2025 rezoning/replat request for a senior-housing-oriented section called "The Highlands" was denied by the Windsor Village Board after resident pushback — worth knowing before John talks to current owners in that specific area.

## C. Development and Phase History

- Plat/development agreement dated effective **August 16, 2018**, recorded with Dane County Register of Deeds August 21, 2018 (Doc #5435598) — per a secondary summary of a Windsor Village Board document; **not independently re-verified from the primary PDF** in this pass (fetch blocked).
- Village of Windsor Board/Trustee resolutions confirm ongoing phased development:
  - 2020-73: Reduction in Letter of Credit for Bear Tree Farms — Phase 2
  - 2022-17: Replat of BTF Lots 88–141 & 146–155
  - 2022-18: Phase 6 Developer's Agreement
  - 2023-11: Phase 7 Amendment to the Development Agreement
  - 2023-108: Alternative water main loop for Phase 7
  - 2025-04: Phase 8 Amendment to Development Agreement
- Construction described by one builder-marketing source as beginning 2016 and continuing "close to 10 years" — **this conflicts slightly** with the 2018 plat-recording date found in Windsor's own resolution titles. Flagging this as an unresolved conflict: either construction/site work began before formal platting, the 2016 figure is simply wrong, or it refers to a related/predecessor project. Do not state a single start year to clients without resolving this.
- **2025 rezoning/replat denial:** Village of Windsor trustees unanimously denied a preliminary replat and rezoning request for "The Highlands" portion of Bear Tree Farms (a proposed senior-housing-oriented section). The request also sought to vacate Honeycomb Lane and Honey Bee Court and portions of Eagle Mound Pass, Ramshorn Drive, and Grouse Woods Road. Existing Bear Tree Farms residents opposed the change at a June 11 public hearing, saying they bought based on the original plans and that the developer hadn't communicated future plans well. Denial reasons cited included condo units/roads encroaching on rights-of-way previously dedicated to the village, and ordinance non-compliance on minimum lot area/width and lot coverage. (Source: hngnews.com/deforest_times article.) This is a live point of resident tension worth knowing before filming or interviewing owners in that section.
- The development plan reportedly includes a small commercial component within/adjacent to Bear Tree Farms: Cortland Park Condominiums, a new Door Creek Church campus, The Yahara River Learning Center, and Paradise Paws Doggie Day Care.

## K. Possible Considerations

- **Municipality mismatch:** buyers may believe they're buying "in DeForest" because of the mailing address and marketing, when the subdivision is actually governed by the Village of Windsor (different local government, ordinances, and likely different municipal tax rate/services than DeForest proper). This is worth clarifying explicitly with any buyer.
- **Active/ongoing development:** as of the most recent confirmed phase (Phase 8, approved 2025), the neighborhood is still being built out. Buyers in earlier phases may be living adjacent to ongoing construction for the foreseeable future.
- **2025 "The Highlands" rezoning denial:** unresolved tension between developer and existing residents over plan changes in one section; worth knowing before engaging owners there, and worth monitoring for a possible revised proposal.
- **HOA fee not confidently sourced** — see Section G; verify before repeating to clients.
- **Builder/phase variability:** because multiple builders (Tim O'Brien, Eldon Homes, custom via Design Custom Homes of Madison) operate in different phases, home style, price point, and possibly even HOA sub-rules could vary meaningfully by phase/section — don't treat the neighborhood as homogenous.
