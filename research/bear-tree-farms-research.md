## A. Expert Summary [CL]

Bear Tree Farms is a large, multi-phase, still-actively-developing single-family (plus some attached/condo) subdivision straddling the DeForest/Windsor/Sun Prairie area of Dane County, WI. Despite being marketed heavily as "DeForest, WI" by builders and portals (likely because it uses a DeForest, WI 53532 mailing address), the subdivision is physically located in, and governed/platted by, the **Village of Windsor** — not the Village/Town limits of DeForest proper. It sits near the boundary between Windsor and Sun Prairie, off Windsor Road and Pederson Crossing Boulevard, with access to US Hwy 51. Schools are DeForest Area School District (Windsor Elementary, DeForest Middle, DeForest High), which is the connective tissue that makes the "DeForest" branding reasonable even though the municipality is Windsor. Development began around 2018 and has proceeded in at least 8 phases through 2025, built out by multiple builders (Tim O'Brien Homes, Eldon Homes, and custom builders via Design Custom Homes of Madison). The neighborhood includes a large (~22-acre) community park with splash pad, sport courts, and trails, plus a small commercial node (Door Creek Church campus, Paradise Paws Doggie Day Care, Yahara River Learning Center, Cortland Park condos). A 2025 rezoning/replat request for a senior-housing-oriented section called "The Highlands" was denied by the Windsor Village Board after resident pushback — worth knowing before talking to current owners in that specific area.

## C. Development and Phase History [CL]

- Plat/development agreement dated effective **August 16, 2018**, recorded with Dane County Register of Deeds August 21, 2018 (Doc #5435598) — per a secondary summary of a Windsor Village Board document; not independently re-verified from the primary PDF.
- Village of Windsor Board/Trustee resolutions confirm ongoing phased development:
  - 2020-73: Reduction in Letter of Credit for Bear Tree Farms — Phase 2
  - 2022-17: Replat of BTF Lots 88–141 & 146–155
  - 2022-18: Phase 6 Developer's Agreement
  - 2023-11: Phase 7 Amendment to the Development Agreement
  - 2023-108: Alternative water main loop for Phase 7
  - 2025-04: Phase 8 Amendment to Development Agreement
- **Conflict — unresolved:** one builder-marketing source describes construction as beginning 2016 ("close to 10 years"), which conflicts with the 2018 plat-recording date found in Windsor's own resolution titles. Either site work predates formal platting, the 2016 figure is simply wrong, or it refers to a related/predecessor project. Do not state a single start year to clients without resolving this.
- **2025 rezoning/replat denial:** Village of Windsor trustees unanimously denied a preliminary replat and rezoning request for "The Highlands" portion of Bear Tree Farms (a proposed senior-housing-oriented section). The request also sought to vacate Honeycomb Lane and Honey Bee Court and portions of Eagle Mound Pass, Ramshorn Drive, and Grouse Woods Road. Existing residents opposed the change at a June 11 public hearing, saying they bought based on the original plans and that the developer hadn't communicated future plans well. Denial reasons cited included condo units/roads encroaching on rights-of-way previously dedicated to the village, and ordinance non-compliance on minimum lot area/width and lot coverage. (Source: hngnews.com/deforest_times.) Live point of resident tension worth knowing before filming/interviewing owners in that section.
- Development plan reportedly includes a small commercial component within/adjacent to Bear Tree Farms: Cortland Park Condominiums, a new Door Creek Church campus, The Yahara River Learning Center, and Paradise Paws Doggie Day Care.
