## ⚠️ THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT ITEM IN THIS BRIEF

**The two legs disagree on whether "Kilkenny Farms" and "Kilkenny Farms West" are the same development or two separate ones — this is not a minor detail, it changes what this entire page should say.**

- **[PP]** Perplexity's brief treats "Kilkenny Farms West" as an **expansion phase of the same Kilkenny Farms development** — it found a Village of Waunakee archive page listing both a "Kilkenny Farms Final Plat (2013)" and a "Kilkenny Farms West – Recorded Final Plat" together, and inferred these are sequential phases of one project.
- **[CL]** Claude's brief treats them as **two entirely separate, unrelated developments** that merely sit near each other and share a naming pattern: Kilkenny Farms (~2014 origin, built out, builder = Classic Custom Homes of Waunakee) vs. Kilkenny Farms West (28-acre Village TID 6 expansion approved 2024–2025 on a 4–3 Village Board vote, developer = Don Tierney/Livable Communities, $2.8M village infrastructure reimbursement, still under active construction with its own forthcoming Aldi).

**Why the evidence favors Claude's account:**
1. Kilkenny Farms West already has its own separate, published Integrity Homes page (`integrityhomeswi.com/waunakee/kilkenny-farms-west/`) and its own separate Airtable tracker record — John's own system already treats them as distinct subdivisions, not phases of one.
2. Claude's sourcing on this specific point is a real news article about a Village Board vote (hngnews.com, with a vote breakdown and dissenting trustees named) — a much stronger primary source than Perplexity's inference from an archive page listing two plat names side by side.
3. The builders are different (Classic Custom Homes vs. Tierney/Espire/Victory Homes), the amenities are different (Kilkenny Farms already has an operating park + pool; Kilkenny Farms West's amenities are still marketed as planned), and one is described as "archived"/built-out while the other is actively under construction with TID financing — that's a lot of independent signals pointing the same direction.

**This is still flagged, not silently resolved, per the merge rules — confirm directly with John or a plat search before publishing.** But if forced to draft now: treat them as two separate subdivisions, and if anything, this conflict itself is good content — Claude's finding #1 in "Only a Local Would Know" (below) suggests exactly this: an explainer clarifying the two is genuinely useful, differentiated content given how tangled the public sourcing is.

## A. Expert Summary

Kilkenny Farms is an established, largely built-out single-family neighborhood in the Village of Waunakee, Dane County — **[PP]** originating with a final plat recorded 2013-05-31 per the Village's own archive, **[CL]** though secondary aggregator sources describe it as "~2014 origin" (treat 2013 as the stronger, primary-sourced date; both may be correct if plat recording and marketing/construction start differed by months). **[PP+CL]** It's anchored by Kilkenny Farms Park (1021 Water Wheel Dr, 5.42 acres, village-owned) with six regulation pickleball courts, and by a small commercial node on Water Wheel Drive (Kwik Trip, Buck & Honey's). **[CL, not found by PP at all]** The neighborhood also has its own private, HOA-affiliated neighborhood pool (kilkennypool.com) — lap pool, activity pool with basketball hoops, beach-entry kiddie pool — seasonal Memorial Day–Labor Day. **[CL]** At least one national aggregator (newhomesource.com) lists the community as "archived," consistent with it being sold-out/built-out rather than actively selling new lots.

See the conflict box above before writing anything about "Kilkenny Farms West" on this page.

## C. Development and Phase History

**[PP+CL, corroborated]** Final plat recorded 2013-05-31 (Village of Waunakee archive). Predominantly single-family homes on individually platted lots (~0.2–0.3 acre range per one confirmed parcel).

**[CL]** Builder confirmed as Classic Custom Homes of Waunakee, Inc. (subdivision listed on their own site, cchofwaunakee.com/kilkenny-farms-subdivision/). No other builder was confirmed as active in Kilkenny Farms proper — Claude's searches kept surfacing Kilkenny Farms *West* builder info (Espire Home Builders, Victory Homes of Wisconsin) instead, which it explicitly excluded as out of scope.

**[PP]** Don Tierney's own site (dontierney.com/neighborhoods/kilkenny-farms) lists "Kilkenny Farms" as one of his "Livable Communities," describing site planning/design/marketing involvement.

**Builder note (not necessarily a conflict):** Tierney's site claiming community-level involvement and Classic Custom Homes' site claiming builder involvement could both be true simultaneously — a site developer plus one of several home builders within the platted lots is a normal arrangement. Confirm the actual division of roles before stating either as "the" developer.

**[CL]** newhomesource.com lists the community as "archived" — a status aggregator sites use for sold-out/no-longer-actively-marketed communities, consistent with a 2013/2014-origin, now-mature neighborhood.

**[PP]** Retired Access Dane parcel record for one lot lists "KILKENNY FARMS LLC" as the historical owner — a development entity name, consistent with (though not proof of) either builder/developer account above.

**Adjacent development — Kilkenny Farms West (separate, per the conflict box above):** **[CL]** A 28-acre Village TID 6 project-plan amendment was approved by the Waunakee Village Board on a 4–3 vote (dissenting: Frye, Kaufmann, McPherson), with the Village committing $2.8M in infrastructure reimbursement to developer Don Tierney/Livable Communities, who committed $5.04M toward infrastructure including a Woodland Drive path. Still under active construction as of 2026, anchored by a forthcoming Aldi. **Do not present any of this TID/construction/Aldi content as belonging to Kilkenny Farms (this brief's subject)** — it's explicitly the adjacent West project.
