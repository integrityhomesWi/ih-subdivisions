## Route / distance to downtown Waunakee

- Multiple aggregator/marketing sources describe Heritage Hills as "within walking distance" of downtown Waunakee's Main Street shops, restaurants, and library. This is repeated marketing language, not a measured distance — **treat "walking distance" as an unverified marketing claim, not a confirmed fact**, until checked on a map from a specific Heritage Hills address. Given the subdivision's southwest corner sits at Main Street and Division Street, and downtown Waunakee's core is also on Main Street/WI-19, a walk of well under a mile from the subdivision's southern edge to downtown is plausible, but this was not independently measured door-to-door in this research pass.

## Route / distance to nearest highway on-ramp

- Downtown Waunakee sits directly on Wisconsin Highway 19 (Main Street). Highway 19 is the local connector linking Waunakee, Sun Prairie, and Watertown.
- Highway 113 (Packers Avenue corridor) passes east of the Village of Waunakee and is the typical route toward Dane County Regional Airport and Madison.
- The North Division Street / STH 19-113 intersection is directly referenced in Village of Waunakee construction notices as adjacent to the Heritage Hills subdivision (site of the Phase 4 blasting notices) — this suggests the subdivision has a genuinely close, arguably on-site, connection point to STH 19/113, though an exact on-ramp distance in miles/minutes was not independently confirmed in this pass.
