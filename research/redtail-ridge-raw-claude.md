## A. Expert Summary

Redtail Ridge is a large, currently-under-construction new-home subdivision in the City of Middleton, WI, sited on former farmland between Pheasant Branch Road and High Road, just north of the established Middleton Hills neighborhood. It is a master-planned, mixed-housing-type community developed by High Road Development Co. (Chad Wuebben, president) with multiple builders — most prominently Encore Homes, Inc., and also Tim O'Brien Homes — building single-family homes, townhomes, small-footprint "cottage" pocket neighborhoods, two-flat condos, live-work units, carriage homes, and large multifamily apartment buildings. City approvals began in 2022 and continued into at least early 2026 (a Phase 3 rezoning hearing was noticed for 2026-02-24), meaning the subdivision is actively being built out in phases, not a finished/static community. Not confident on total build-out completion date.

## C. Development and Phase History

- **2022:** High Road Development Co. (Chad Wuebben) brought the Redtail Ridge concept to the City of Middleton Plan Commission; a public hearing was held November 22, 2022, per Middleton Times-Tribune coverage. Early coverage (Madison.com, ibmadison.com) describes the project as converting farmland north of Middleton Hills, between High Road and Pheasant Branch Road, into a mixed residential/commercial neighborhood.
- **2022 GIP application:** A "Park Redtail Ridge" General Implementation Plan (GIP) application is dated December 30, 2022, per a City of Middleton meeting document title — indicates park planning was part of the earliest approvals package.
- **Feb 2023 (reported):** Rezoning approval reported via Plan Commission action — not independently confirmed beyond a derived date in search summaries; flag as needing primary-source confirmation.
- **Preliminary and final plats:** City of Middleton meeting-document titles reference a "Redtail Ridge — 1st Addition" final plat, a Phase I final-plat submittal with engineering staff comments, and a preliminary plat with staff comments — confirming the subdivision has been built out in numbered phases/additions, consistent with normal Wisconsin subdivision platting practice. Exact phase boundaries and dates were not retrievable (WebFetch to cityofmiddleton.us was blocked).
- **2026:** A "NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING — Request to Rezone Lots 123-318 and Outlot 23" tied to "Redtail Ridge Ph 3" is dated 2026-02-24, meaning Phase 3 rezoning was still moving through city approvals as of early 2026. **This means, as of the current date (2026-08-17), Redtail Ridge is an active, multi-phase, partially built-out subdivision — not a finished neighborhood.** Some product types (notably the apartment buildings and later single-family phases) may not yet be complete.
- **Construction status:** A blog post ("Retiring Guy's Digest") documents an October 2025 construction site visit to the apartment component, consistent with ongoing build-out.
- City park construction on the ~8 acres dedicated to the City is reported (via a search-engine synthesis of a city GIP document) as not expected to begin until 2027, with planning starting mid-to-late 2026 — **not confident**, this is a summarized inference from a document title/snippet, not a direct read of the document (blocked). ## K. Possible Considerations

- **Active construction zone.** As of 2026-08-17, the subdivision has phases still in city approval (Phase 3 rezoning hearing noticed for Feb 2026) and at least one major component (apartments) still under construction as of an October 2025 site-visit blog post. Buyers should expect ongoing construction noise/traffic and changing streetscape for the foreseeable future.
- **Multiple, possibly-differing HOA/association structures** across single-family, townhome, cottage, and condo product types — a buyer's fee and rules will depend heavily on which specific product/section they buy into. Do not quote one fee for the whole neighborhood.
- **School boundary uncertainty at the edges of a large, still-growing development** — verify by address every time.
- **Density and mixed use.** With ~420–444 apartment units, multiple multifamily buildings, and eventual commercial/live-work components, Redtail Ridge is materially denser and more mixed-use than a typical single-family subdivision — this may be a draw for some buyers and a drawback for others expecting a purely single-family environment.
- **City parkland not yet built.** The ~8 acres dedicated to the City of Middleton reportedly won't see construction until 2027 at the earliest (unconfirmed) — buyers moving in now should not expect a finished city park on day one.
- **Conflicting unit-count figures** (e.g., 444 vs. ~420 apartment units) suggest the original 2022 proposal and the as-building-out reality may differ — final built unit counts should be confirmed against current city records, not the original proposal.

## L. Comparison With Nearby Subdivisions

- **Middleton Hills** (immediately south of Redtail Ridge): An established New Urbanist neighborhood begun in the late 1990s, now 400+ detached homes plus condos, walkable street grid, two established neighborhood parks, and close proximity (~15-minute walk) to the Middleton Hills shopping/dining node. Detached homes there start around $400,000-ish and range up toward $1.5M for larger homes (dated to this research's retrieval — not sold-price data). By contrast, Redtail Ridge is newer, still under active construction, has a much wider mix of housing types (including apartments and cottages), and does not yet have the mature tree canopy or fully built-out commercial node that Middleton Hills has.
- **Eagle Trace / Crest at Eagle Trace** — confirmed via this research to be a **separate** subdivision built by Veridian Homes, with a Verona mailing address (10129 White Fox Lane) despite being in the broader Middleton-area market and zoned to MCPASD. **This is exactly the kind of name-collision risk flagged in the task instructions — Redtail Ridge (Encore Homes/High Road Development, Middleton address, off Pheasant Branch/High Road) is a distinct project from Eagle Trace/Crest at Eagle Trace (Veridian Homes, Verona address).** Do not conflate them in any client-facing material.
