## A. Expert Summary [CL]

Redtail Ridge is a large, currently-under-construction new-home subdivision in the City of Middleton, WI, sited on former farmland between Pheasant Branch Road and High Road, just north of the established Middleton Hills neighborhood. It is a master-planned, mixed-housing-type community developed by High Road Development Co. (Chad Wuebben, president) with multiple builders — most prominently Encore Homes, Inc., and also Tim O'Brien Homes — building single-family homes, townhomes, small-footprint "cottage" pocket neighborhoods, two-flat condos, live-work units, carriage homes, and large multifamily apartment buildings. City approvals began in 2022 and continued into at least early 2026 (a Phase 3 rezoning hearing was noticed for 2026-02-24), meaning the subdivision is actively being built out in phases, not a finished/static community. Not confident on total build-out completion date.

## C. Development and Phase History [CL]

- **2022:** High Road Development Co. (Chad Wuebben) brought the Redtail Ridge concept to the City of Middleton Plan Commission; public hearing held November 22, 2022 (Middleton Times-Tribune coverage). Early coverage (Madison.com, ibmadison.com) describes farmland north of Middleton Hills, between High Road and Pheasant Branch Road, being converted to a mixed residential/commercial neighborhood.
- **2022 GIP application:** A "Park Redtail Ridge" General Implementation Plan (GIP) application dated December 30, 2022, per a City of Middleton meeting document title — park planning was part of the earliest approvals package.
- **Feb 2023 (reported):** Rezoning approval reported via Plan Commission action — not independently confirmed beyond a derived date in search summaries; flag as needing primary-source confirmation.
- **Preliminary and final plats:** City meeting-document titles reference a "Redtail Ridge — 1st Addition" final plat, a Phase I final-plat submittal with engineering staff comments, and a preliminary plat with staff comments — confirms numbered phases/additions consistent with normal WI platting practice. Exact phase boundaries/dates not retrievable (WebFetch blocked).
- **2026:** A "NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING — Request to Rezone Lots 123-318 and Outlot 23" tied to "Redtail Ridge Ph 3" dated 2026-02-24 — Phase 3 rezoning still moving through city approvals as of early 2026. As of 2026-08-17, Redtail Ridge is an active, multi-phase, partially built-out subdivision, not finished. Some product types (notably apartments and later single-family phases) may not yet be complete.
- **Construction status:** A blog post ("Retiring Guy's Digest") documents an October 2025 construction site visit to the apartment component, consistent with ongoing build-out.
- City park construction on the ~8 dedicated acres is reported (via summarized document snippet) as not expected to begin until 2027, with planning starting mid-to-late 2026 — **not confident**, a summarized inference from a document title/snippet, not a direct document read (blocked). Verify directly with City of Middleton Parks Dept.

## K. Possible Considerations [CL]

- **Active construction zone.** As of 2026-08-17, phases still in city approval (Phase 3 rezoning hearing noticed for Feb 2026) and at least one major component (apartments) still under construction as of an October 2025 site-visit blog post. Expect ongoing construction noise/traffic and a changing streetscape for the foreseeable future.
- **Multiple, possibly-differing HOA/association structures** across single-family, townhome, cottage, and condo product types — a buyer's fee and rules depend heavily on which specific product/section they buy into. Do not quote one fee for the whole neighborhood.
- **School boundary uncertainty at the edges of a large, still-growing development** — verify by address every time.
- **Density and mixed use.** With ~420–444 apartment units, multiple multifamily buildings, and eventual commercial/live-work components, Redtail Ridge is materially denser and more mixed-use than a typical single-family subdivision — a draw for some buyers, a drawback for others expecting a purely single-family environment.
- **City parkland not yet built.** The ~8 acres dedicated to the City of Middleton reportedly won't see construction until 2027 at the earliest (unconfirmed) — buyers moving in now should not expect a finished city park on day one.
- **Conflicting unit-count figures** (e.g., 444 vs. ~420 apartment units) suggest the original 2022 proposal and the as-building-out reality may differ — confirm final built unit counts against current city records, not the original proposal.

## L. Comparison With Nearby Subdivisions [CL]

- **Middleton Hills** (immediately south of Redtail Ridge): An established New Urbanist neighborhood begun in the late 1990s, now 400+ detached homes plus condos, walkable street grid, two established neighborhood parks, close proximity (~15-minute walk) to the Middleton Hills shopping/dining node. Detached homes there start around $400,000-ish and range up toward $1.5M for larger homes (dated to retrieval — not sold-price data). By contrast, Redtail Ridge is newer, still under active construction, has a much wider mix of housing types (including apartments and cottages), and does not yet have the mature tree canopy or fully built-out commercial node that Middleton Hills has.
- **Eagle Trace / Crest at Eagle Trace** — confirmed to be a **separate** subdivision built by Veridian Homes, with a Verona mailing address (10129 White Fox Lane) despite being in the broader Middleton-area market and zoned to MCPASD. **Name-collision risk flagged explicitly by the source leg** — Redtail Ridge (Encore Homes/High Road Development, Middleton address, off Pheasant Branch/High Road) is a distinct project from Eagle Trace/Crest at Eagle Trace (Veridian Homes, Verona address). Do not conflate them in any client-facing material.
