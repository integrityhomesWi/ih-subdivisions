## A. Expert Summary

Midvale Heights is a large, established, primarily post-WWII residential neighborhood on Madison's near-west side — **not a new-construction subdivision** in the sense of the Waunakee projects this repo otherwise covers. It is reportedly Madison's largest neighborhood by land area (857 acres per one source) and was built out from the late 1940s through 1970, absorbing several smaller original plats (Oakville Heights, Kenmore, Hillview, Third Oak, Coney Weston) under the "Midvale Heights" name. It has an active, long-running neighborhood association (Midvale Heights Community Association, founded 1954) rather than a private developer HOA. [CL]
