## A. Expert Summary

Midvale Heights is a large, established, primarily post-WWII residential neighborhood on Madison's near-west side — **not a new-construction subdivision** in the sense of the Waunakee projects this repo otherwise covers. It is Madison's largest neighborhood by land area (857 acres per one source) and was built out from the late 1940s through 1970, absorbing several smaller original plats (Oakville Heights, Kenmore, Hillview, Third Oak, Coney Weston) under the "Midvale Heights" name. It has an active, long-running neighborhood association (Midvale Heights Community Association, founded 1954) rather than a private developer HOA. **Important caution carried over from this repo's Kilkenny/Southbridge naming-collision precedent: confirm any specific claim is about Madison's Midvale Heights (Dane County, WI) and not a same-named subdivision elsewhere in the US** — this name is generic enough that it likely exists in other states; nothing in this pass surfaced a Wisconsin/Madison mix-up, but the risk should stay flagged until the Perplexity leg cross-checks it.

## M. Frequently Asked Questions

No — it is one of Madison's older, established, primarily post-WWII neighborhoods (homes mainly built 1947–1970), not a new-construction development. This is a meaningfully different product than the newer Waunakee subdivisions this repo otherwise covers.

No confirmed mandatory HOA. There is a long-running, volunteer-run **Midvale Heights Community Association** (founded 1954), but whether it charges dues or enforces architectural rules was **not confirmed** this pass — verify at midvaleheights.org before stating anything definitive.
