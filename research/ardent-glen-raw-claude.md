## A. Expert Summary

Ardent Glen is a new-construction subdivision built by **Veridian Homes** on Verona's west side, on what was locally known as the "Marty Property" before development. It sits north of County Highway PD (McKee Road) and west of Shady Oak Lane, near the intersection of County Road PD/McKee Road and Star Light Drive. The Verona Common Council approved the preliminary/final plat on **December 19, 2022** (4–2 vote), following a Plan Commission review on **December 5, 2022**. The approved plan rezoned lots 1–406 to Mixed Residential and outlots 1–25 to Public Institutional, from Rural Agricultural. Total planned build-out is **258 single-family homes and 148 twin homes (406 units)**. First move-ins were reported for spring 2024, with phased releases continuing since (a "phase releases" builder blog post dated October 2024 was found, indicating the neighborhood is still building out as of that date — current phase status not confirmed for 2026). A neighborhood park ("Ardent Glen Park") exists and had playground equipment installed per a City of Verona social post; a City planning document also describes a planned park in the southeast portion of the site with active/passive recreation and a north-south pedestrian path, subject to Park Board approval — it is not fully clear from available sources whether the completed park matches that full planned scope. This is a Verona (Dane County) subdivision, distinct from any similarly-named development elsewhere; no conflicting "Ardent Glen" elsewhere in the Madison area surfaced in research.

## C. Development and Phase History

- **Pre-2022:** Site known informally as the "Marty Property," zoned Rural Agricultural. A related earlier planning item titled "Marty property – Shady Oak Lane Planning Report" also surfaced in search results, suggesting city review activity predates the final December 2022 approval — exact timeline of intermediate steps not fully mapped from available snippets.
- **Dec. 5, 2022:** City of Verona Plan Commission reviewed the Ardent Glen planning report.
- **Dec. 19, 2022:** Verona Common Council approved the preliminary and final plat for Ardent Glen, 4–2 (Alders Kate Cronin, District 3, and Mara Helmke, District 2, voted against). Council-level opposition centered on traffic impact to Shady Oak Lane and the Town of Verona, distance from Verona's city center, and added density.
- **Zoning action:** Lots 1–406 rezoned to Mixed Residential (MR); Outlots 1–25 to Public Institutional (PI).
- **2023:** Veridian Homes published a "Welcome Home to Ardent Glen" introductory blog post (Nov. 16, 2023), suggesting active marketing/pre-sales began around that time.
