About the Neighborhood

Built on the Marty Property.
Still Taking Shape.

Ardent Glen is a new-construction neighborhood on Verona, Wisconsin's west side, built by Veridian Homes on land long known locally as the "Marty Property." The Verona Common Council approved the preliminary and final plat on December 19, 2022, by a 4–2 vote, following Plan Commission review two weeks earlier. The approval rezoned lots 1–406 to Mixed Residential and outlots 1–25 to Public Institutional, replacing the site's prior Rural Agricultural zoning.

The neighborhood sits north of County Highway PD (McKee Road) and west of Shady Oak Lane, near the intersection of McKee Road and Star Light Drive, in ZIP code 53593. Planned build-out is 258 single-family homes and 148 twin homes — 406 units total — with first move-ins reported for spring 2024 and phased releases continuing into at least September 2025 per a dated Veridian marketing brochure. How many of the 406 planned homes are built, sold, or still available as of August 2026 has not been independently confirmed — ask John for the current picture.

"Longtime Verona residents still call it the Marty Property — the rezoning changed the map, not the name locals actually use."

Ardent Glen shouldn't be confused with Whispering Coves, a separate mixed single-family/condo development just south of McKee Road that has built out more slowly since breaking ground in 2020 — the two sit close enough geographically that buyers researching this corridor sometimes mix them up. The 2022 approval wasn't unanimous, either: two Verona alders voted against the plat, citing traffic impact on Shady Oak Lane and the Town of Verona, plus concerns about added density from the twin-home component.
