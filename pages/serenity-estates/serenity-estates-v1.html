About the Neighborhood

A Plat Redesigned to
Protect the Wetlands

Serenity Estates — often marketed as Serenity Estates at Token Creek, and referenced in Sun Prairie municipal records as Token Creek Serenity Estates — sits on the north side of Sun Prairie, Wisconsin, directly along Token Creek and bordering a roughly 190-acre conservancy corridor. Developer Coris Development Group rezoned the land to Suburban Residential (SR-4) in June 2021, and the City Council approved the final plat on October 19, 2021, without discussion — the quiet ending to a process that took an unusual turn along the way.

The original plan extended Lonnie Lane west across mapped wetlands. City planners postponed action on the final plat twice — at both the August and September 2021 Plan Commission meetings — while the developer redesigned the street layout to avoid disturbing the wetlands. The amended plat replaced that crossing with two cul-de-sacs off Lonnie Lane and a third off St. Patrick's Way, plus a dedicated pedestrian pathway and bridge crossing the wetlands between Lonnie Court and St. Patrick's Way. The result: a cul-de-sac-heavy street pattern, and roughly 18 of the plat's 35.22 acres set aside as permanent environmental corridor, wetland, floodplain, and trail.

"The road layout changed mid-approval specifically to avoid disturbing the wetlands — nearly half the platted acreage ended up as permanent public open space instead of additional lots."

Serenity Estates is informally split into two sections with different covenant minimums and price points: an Eastern Plat (modern-farmhouse styling, 1,600-square-foot minimum home size) and a Western Plat (creek-adjacent, upscale styling with stone, stucco, and walkout basements, 2,000-square-foot minimum). William Ryan Homes is the primary community builder; Victory Homes of Wisconsin builds a custom "Cedar" model. Covenants recorded December 23, 2021 (revised January 5, 2022) require the garage to stay visually secondary to the home and the entry porch closest to the street.

Developer

Coris Development Group, LLC — rezoned to SR-4 June 2021; final plat approved by Sun Prairie City Council October 19, 2021.

Location

North side of Sun Prairie, along Token Creek, south of Stonehaven Drive, in the Highway 51 corridor. Confirmed streets: Stonehaven Drive, St. Patrick's Way, Lonnie Court, Lonnie Lane.

Open Space

~18 of the plat's 35.22 acres (about half) are dedicated public environmental corridor, wetlands, floodplain, and trail — a condition of plat approval, not a built recreational amenity.

Price Range

Builder-quoted, as of August 2026: Eastern section roughly $650K–$700K; Western section $1M+. Sold-price data is pending SCWMLS.

Why People Choose It

What Draws Buyers to Serenity Estates

Eight things grounded directly in the documented plat history and covenants — not promotional adjectives.

01

Nearly Half the Plat Is Permanent Open Space

Roughly 18 of the subdivision's 35.22 platted acres are dedicated environmental corridor, wetland, floodplain, and trail — a condition of the approved plat, not a marketing promise. For a 39-lot subdivision, that's an unusually large public-land dedication relative to lot count.

02

A Layout That Keeps Traffic Out

The final plat was redesigned specifically to avoid a wetland crossing, replacing a through-street with cul-de-sacs off Lonnie Lane and St. Patrick's Way — a documented, cul-de-sac-heavy street pattern rather than an assumption about "quiet streets."

03

A Named Bridge Over the Wetlands

A pedestrian pathway and bridge cross the wetlands between Lonnie Court and St. Patrick's Way — a real, built public improvement required as part of plat approval, not just creek adjacency on paper.

04

Token Creek and a ~190-Acre Conservancy

The subdivision borders a roughly 190-acre conservancy corridor along Token Creek, a documented contributor to the Yahara River/Lake Mendota watershed, with Token Creek County Park nearby.

05

A Real Choice of Builders

William Ryan Homes serves as the primary community builder, while Victory Homes of Wisconsin offers a custom "Cedar" model — a mixed production-and-custom builder roster most single-builder subdivisions can't offer.

06

Architectural Standards With Teeth

Covenants require the garage to stay visually secondary to the home and the entry porch to sit closest to the street — a specific, enforceable design standard, not a vague "quality construction" claim.

07

A Small, Low-Density Plat by Design

39 single-family lots on 35.22 acres is small compared to nearby master-planned developments like The Reserve (313 lots). Buyers get a quieter, lower-density feel — without the dedicated park or future school site that comes with a larger development.

08

Two Sections, Two Price Points, One HOA

The Eastern Plat (1,600 sq ft minimum, builder-quoted roughly $650K–$700K) and Western Plat (2,000 sq ft minimum, builder-quoted $1M+) give buyers real range within one association — as long as they know which section they're looking at.

Is this the same as "The Reserve"?

No evidence of one was found. The documented amenity here is trail and pedestrian-bridge access to dedicated open space, not a built recreational facility like a pool or clubhouse.
How many lots are in Serenity Estates?

Yes — Serenity Estates Homeowners Association, Inc. Dues are not publicly confirmed; ask the listing agent or management company for current figures.
Who's building here?
William Ryan Homes is the primary community builder, and Victory Homes of Wisconsin builds a custom "Cedar" model. At least one active listing has also named Turn Key Construction — possibly a third, spec/custom builder; this is not yet confirmed.
Is this the same as "The Reserve"?
No — The Reserve is a separate, much larger (313-lot) development nearby, north of Stonehaven Drive between Amanda Avenue and School Street. Don't conflate the two.
Is there a pool or clubhouse?
No evidence of one was found. The documented amenity here is trail and pedestrian-bridge access to dedicated open space, not a built recreational facility like a pool or clubhouse.
How many lots are in Serenity Estates?
39 single-family lots plus 6 outlots on approximately 35.22 acres, roughly 18 of which are dedicated public open space.
Why do some homes have a smaller minimum size than others?
The subdivision is informally split into an "Eastern" section (1,600 sq ft minimum home size, builder-quoted roughly $650K–$700K) and a "Western" section (2,000 sq ft minimum, builder-quoted $1M+), with different covenant minimums and price points within the same plat.
What are homes selling for in Serenity Estates?
Sold-price, median-price, and days-on-market figures are pending SCWMLS data. Builder-quoted new-construction pricing, as of August 2026, has run roughly $650,000–$700,000 in the Eastern section and $1 million-plus in the Western section — that's builder pricing, not closed-sale data.
