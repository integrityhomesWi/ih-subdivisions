What is Castle Crest in Waunakee, Wisconsin?

Castle Crest is an established residential neighborhood near the center of Waunakee, Wisconsin, in Dane County, bordered by Knightsbridge Road, South Street, and Winston Way. Built mainly in the 1970s and early 1980s, it features one- and two-story homes on tree-lined lots within walking distance of downtown Waunakee, the Waunakee Public Library, and the Rotary Walk path along Sixmile Creek. About the Neighborhood

Established, Walkable,
Already at Village Center.

Castle Crest is one of Waunakee's older established neighborhoods, sitting close enough to the village center that a walk downtown is a genuine option, not a marketing line. The neighborhood is bordered by Knightsbridge Road to the north, South Street to the west, and Winston Way to the south and east — a compact, well-defined footprint of quiet residential streets a few blocks from Main Street.

The housing stock reflects its age: a mix of one- and two-story homes built primarily in the 1970s and early 1980s, set on shaded, tree-lined lots that have had five decades to mature. That's a different character than Waunakee's newer west-side subdivisions — no active construction, no half-finished streetscapes, just an established neighborhood that's been part of the village fabric for generations.

"Castle Crest is close enough to downtown Waunakee that residents can walk to the library — that's a genuinely rare thing to find in a single-family neighborhood."

Recreation is built into daily life here. The Waunakee Rotary Walk — a path around a neighborhood pond, completed by the Rotary Club in 2008 with a bridge donated by Endres Manufacturing — is a short stroll away, and Sixmile Creek offers a jogging route into Waunakee Village Park. Downtown Waunakee's shops, restaurants, and the public library are all within easy walking or biking range.

Parks & Recreation

Outdoor Life in Castle Crest

Castle Crest's outdoor amenities are less about an in-neighborhood park and more about direct walking access to the village's public recreation — the Rotary Walk, Sixmile Creek, and downtown Waunakee itself.

🌊

Waunakee Rotary Walk

A walking path around a neighborhood pond, completed by the Waunakee Rotary Club in 2008 — a sandy contractor-built path with a bridge over Sixmile Creek donated by Endres Manufacturing. The Rotary has since added lighting and a pier. A short walk from Castle Crest.

🏞️

Sixmile Creek & Waunakee Village Park

A jogging and walking route follows Sixmile Creek from the neighborhood toward Waunakee Village Park — one of the village's central public parks.

📚

Waunakee Public Library

201 N Madison Street — within walking distance of most Castle Crest homes, per multiple local real estate sources. Programs, community meeting space, and South Central Library System access.

🏛️

Downtown Waunakee

Main Street's shops, restaurants, and the Wednesday farmers market are all within easy walking or biking range — one of the closest downtown-proximity stories of any Waunakee neighborhood.

Castle Crest FAQ

Where is the Castle Crest neighborhood in Waunakee?
Castle Crest sits near the center of Waunakee, Wisconsin, bordered by Knightsbridge Road to the north, South Street to the west, and Winston Way to the south and east — an easy walk to downtown Waunakee and the Waunakee Public Library.
Is Castle Crest the same neighborhood as Castle Creek?
No. Castle Crest and Castle Creek are two separate Waunakee neighborhoods with similar names — an easy mix-up in MLS records. Castle Crest is the established, downtown-adjacent neighborhood covered on this page (Knightsbridge/South Street/Winston Way). Castle Creek is a different neighborhood near Bolz Conservancy and Sixmile Creek, closer to the Six Mile Creek and Westport side of the village.
How old are the homes in Castle Crest?
Most homes in Castle Crest were built in the 1970s and early 1980s — one- and two-story houses on tree-lined, shaded lots. It's one of the older established neighborhoods inside the village rather than a new-construction subdivision.
