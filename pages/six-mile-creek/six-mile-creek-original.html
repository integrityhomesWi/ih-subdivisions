What is Six Mile Creek in Waunakee, Wisconsin?

Six Mile Creek is an established residential neighborhood on the east side of Waunakee, Wisconsin, in Dane County, built along the Six Mile Creek corridor and the eastern edge of The Meadows of Sixmile Creek golf course. Homes date mainly to the 1990s and 2000s, and the neighborhood centers on Prairie Park, with tennis, basketball, and open fields built in. Recent single-family sales closed between $625,000 and $670,000 over the past 12 months per SCWMLS data, with nearby condos in the $325,000–$355,000 range. Waunakee Community School District serves the neighborhood, with Prairie Elementary within walking distance of many homes.

About the Neighborhood

An Established Neighborhood,
Already Finished.

Six Mile Creek sits on the east side of Waunakee, running along the Six Mile Creek corridor and the eastern edge of The Meadows of Sixmile Creek golf course. The subdivision was part of a larger development concept platted alongside the course — roughly 550 single-family homes in total — which is why the area carries a golf-community identity even though not every home backs up to a fairway. (You'll see it both ways: the entrance monument and golf course spell it "Sixmile Creek" as one word, while most buyers and the MLS search "Six Mile Creek" — they're the same neighborhood.)

What sets it apart from Waunakee's newer west-side subdivisions is simple: it's already done. Where a new-construction neighborhood is still filling in lots and finishing infrastructure, Six Mile Creek has mature streets, settled landscaping, and neighborhood amenities that are already in place. The housing stock leans toward larger move-up homes from the 1990s and 2000s — open floor plans, updated finishes, and three-car garages are common.

"Six Mile Creek already feels finished — mature streets, established landscaping, and a real neighborhood park, without the construction-zone wait."

The setting gives the neighborhood a green, open feel: conservancy space, the creek corridor, and the golf course frame the edges, while the interior streets stay quiet and residential. You're a short drive from downtown Waunakee and have straightforward routes toward Madison and the surrounding employment centers.

Common Questions

Six Mile Creek FAQ

Where is the Six Mile Creek neighborhood in Waunakee?
Six Mile Creek is on the east side of Waunakee, running along the Six Mile Creek corridor and the eastern edge of The Meadows of Sixmile Creek golf course, with easy access to downtown Waunakee and Madison-area commuting routes.
What is the main amenity in Six Mile Creek?
Prairie Park is the centerpiece. It's built into the neighborhood with tennis and basketball courts, open fields for soccer and lacrosse, playground equipment, and walking-trail connections from multiple sides of the subdivision.
Are the homes on the golf course?
Some are near it, but many sit across the corridor from the course. The Meadows of Sixmile Creek was originally part of the development plat, so the area has a golf-community feel even though not every home is on a fairway.
How much do homes in Six Mile Creek cost?
Recent single-family sales closed in roughly the $625,000–$670,000 range, while nearby condos and village-home-style units have sold around $325,000–$355,000 — a lower-maintenance entry point into the same address. Pricing varies by condition, updates, and lot, so ask John for live comps.
What kind of homes are in Six Mile Creek?
Mostly larger resale homes from the 1990s and 2000s, with open floor plans, updated finishes, and three-car garages common. It reads as a move-up neighborhood rather than a starter-home area, with condos and village-home-style units offering a lower-maintenance option nearby.
What schools serve Six Mile Creek?
The neighborhood is in the Waunakee Community School District, with Prairie Elementary within walking distance of many homes. Depending on the address, students may also attend Waunakee Intermediate, Waunakee Middle School, and Waunakee Community High School. Always verify the exact attendance area by property address.
