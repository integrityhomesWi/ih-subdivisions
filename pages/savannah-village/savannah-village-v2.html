About the Neighborhood

An Established Neighborhood,
Built Across Five Additions

Savannah Village is a mature, apparently fully built-out subdivision on the south/southeast side of the Village of Waunakee — the closest of Waunakee's neighborhoods to Madison researched to date. It is not one single plat: it comprises at least five named additions — Montondon, Monticello, Hanover, Eldorado Woods, and Woodbridge — each potentially carrying its own recorded covenants and restrictions. Buyers and agents should never assume a rule confirmed for one addition automatically applies to another.

The Monticello Addition has a confirmed builder relationship: Premier Builders' own site lists "Savannah Village – Monticello Addition" as a featured neighborhood, marketed on its outdoor-sports amenities and close access to Arboretum Elementary, with inventory handled through Livable Communities by Don Tierney — the same development office behind Westbridge, Golden Ponds, Southbridge, and the Kilkenny Farms subdivisions elsewhere in Waunakee. The builder/declarant relationship for the other four additions has not been separately confirmed. No addition currently shows active, available lots — this is a resale-only neighborhood today, not a new-construction one.

Savannah Village has one of the better-documented amenities found across any Waunakee neighborhood in this research: the Savannah Village Neighborhood Pool, run by a real, financially transparent 501(c)(7) nonprofit tax-exempt since 2007, with public IRS filings showing healthy net assets and an active current season. A separate, official Village of Waunakee park — Savannah Park — is also confirmed to exist, though its exact street address remains an open question (see the Parks section below).

"Savannah Village is really five neighborhoods sharing one name. What's true for the Monticello Addition — its builder, its marketing, its proximity claims — isn't automatically true for Hanover or Woodbridge next door."

What Savannah Village does not yet have confirmed is a covenant-enforcement homeowners association distinct from the pool nonprofit — no Wisconsin corporate record, management company, or dues figure for one was found in this research. That doesn't necessarily mean there isn't one; it means it hasn't been confirmed, and shouldn't be assumed either way before closing.

Know before you go: A proposed solar-energy project, Six Mile Solar (OneEnergy Renewables, 6 megawatts on 30 acres), sits on Kennedy Road directly behind the Savannah Village neighborhood. Local officials denied the project's permit in February 2026, but the developer sued to challenge that denial in Dane County Circuit Court in March 2026, and the case remains unresolved. This is a legitimate, currently open issue worth discussing directly with any buyer considering the neighborhood's southeast-facing lots — not something to describe as fully settled in either direction.

Savannah Park Facility Confirmed · Address Unresolved

Savannah Park is a real, officially named Village of Waunakee park facility. Reported features include paved, lighted walking trails and a roller-hockey area, per an independent real-estate blog citing Village planning documents. Its exact street address is unresolved — this research found conflicting claims of 1202 Bluebird Trail and 1202 Arboretum Drive — so neither is presented here as settled, and the feature list has not been field-verified.

🎾

Hanover Park (Reported) Confirmation Needed

A separate nearby Village park reported to hold tennis and ball-field facilities distinct from Savannah Park. Plausible — Waunakee has several small neighborhood parks — but no independent source confirming this park's existence or exact amenities was found this research pass.

🏊

Savannah Village Neighborhood Pool Verified — Active Nonprofit

A real, financially transparent 501(c)(7) nonprofit, tax-exempt since March 2007, with public IRS filings. Membership requires a $1,000 one-time initiation fee plus an annual renewal fee (amount stated on the application); guest passes and babysitter passes are also available. Whether membership extends beyond Savannah Village residents isn't confirmed.

☀️

Six Mile Solar (Proposed, Adjacent) Unresolved — Active Litigation

Not a neighborhood amenity, but directly relevant to anyone considering a southeast-facing lot: a proposed 6-megawatt, 30-acre solar project sits on Kennedy Road immediately behind Savannah Village. Denied by the Waunakee/Westport Joint Plan Commission in February 2026, the project is now the subject of a developer lawsuit in Dane County Circuit Court, unresolved as of this research.

August 2026

Savannah Village trips people up because it's really five separate additions under one name — Montondon, Monticello, Hanover, Eldorado Woods, and Woodbridge. I'll show a buyer a home in Hanover and they'll assume it's built to the same covenants as the house they liked in Montondon down the street. It usually isn't. I tell every buyer here: find out which addition you're actually in before you assume anything about the rules.

August 2026

People ask me about the HOA in Savannah Village a lot, and the honest answer is I haven't found a standalone covenant-enforcement association collecting dues here. What's well documented is the pool association, which is its own real, financially transparent nonprofit — but that's a different thing from the HOA a buyer might be used to in a newer subdivision. If dues and architectural control matter to you, confirm directly with the seller or a title company before assuming either way.

August 2026

The Six Mile Solar project on Kennedy Road comes up more than you'd expect, especially with buyers looking at the southeast-facing lots. The permit was denied back in February 2026, but the developer took it to Dane County Circuit Court in March, and that case is still open. I bring it up myself with anyone considering those lots rather than waiting for them to ask.

August 2026

The trade-off with Savannah Village is pretty simple: you're closer to Madison than almost anywhere else in Waunakee, and there's no construction to wait out. What you give up compared to a newer subdivision like Golden Ponds is that everything here is a resale — no builder to pick a floor plan from, no brand-new amenities. For a buyer who wants Waunakee schools and a shorter commute more than they want new construction, it's a real option worth a look.

Is Savannah Village still being actively built?
No. Savannah Village appears fully built out. It comprises five named additions — Montondon, Monticello, Hanover, Eldorado Woods, and Woodbridge — and the one addition with a confirmed builder (Premier Builders' Monticello Addition) currently shows no available lots on the builder's own site.
Who built Savannah Village?
The Monticello Addition was built by Premier Builders, marketed through Livable Communities by Don Tierney — the same development office behind Westbridge, Golden Ponds, Southbridge, and the Kilkenny Farms subdivisions elsewhere in Waunakee. The builder/declarant for the other four named additions has not been separately confirmed.
What school does Savannah Village feed into?
Waunakee Community School District. Arboretum Elementary very likely serves the neighborhood — two independent sources, a school-directory site and Premier Builders' own marketing for the Monticello Addition, both associate Savannah Village with Arboretum Elementary. Confirm a specific address with the WCSD Registrar, (608) 849-2000, before writing an offer.
Is there a pool in Savannah Village?
Yes — the Savannah Village Neighborhood Pool, operated by a real, financially active 501(c)(7) nonprofit tax-exempt since 2007. Membership requires a $1,000 one-time initiation fee plus an annual renewal fee (amount stated on the application).
Is there a homeowners association, and what does it cost?
The neighborhood pool has its own well-documented nonprofit association, but a separate covenant-enforcement HOA handling architectural control and general dues could not be identified in this research. Confirm directly with the seller or a title company before assuming either way.
How far is Savannah Village from Madison?
About 24 minutes and 10.2 miles to Capitol Square by routed driving time — noticeably closer than most other Waunakee subdivisions. A commonly repeated marketing claim of "~15 minutes to Madison" does not match this routed figure and shouldn't be relied on.
What parks are near Savannah Village?
Savannah Park is a confirmed, official Village of Waunakee facility, reported to include paved, lighted walking trails and a roller-hockey area — though its exact street address is still being confirmed between two conflicting sources. A separately reported "Hanover Park" with tennis and ball-field facilities has not yet been independently verified.
Is there a solar farm proposed near Savannah Village?
Yes. Six Mile Solar, a proposed 6-megawatt, 30-acre project by OneEnergy Renewables, sits on Kennedy Road directly behind the neighborhood. The Waunakee/Westport Joint Plan Commission denied the project's conditional use permit on February 23, 2026, but the developer filed a Writ of Certiorari in Dane County Circuit Court on March 25, 2026 challenging that denial — the case remains unresolved. This is worth discussing directly with buyers considering the neighborhood's southeast-facing lots.
How many additions make up Savannah Village, and does that matter?
At least five: Montondon, Monticello, Hanover, Eldorado Woods, and Woodbridge. It matters because recorded covenants, dues, and restrictions may differ by addition — a rule confirmed for one should never be assumed to apply to all of them.

Common Questions

Savannah Village FAQ

✍️ FAQ ▾
Is Savannah Village still being actively built?
No. Savannah Village appears fully built out. It comprises five named additions — Montondon, Monticello, Hanover, Eldorado Woods, and Woodbridge — and the one addition with a confirmed builder (Premier Builders' Monticello Addition) currently shows no available lots on the builder's own site.
Who built Savannah Village?
The Monticello Addition was built by Premier Builders, marketed through Livable Communities by Don Tierney — the same development office behind Westbridge, Golden Ponds, Southbridge, and the Kilkenny Farms subdivisions elsewhere in Waunakee. The builder/declarant for the other four named additions has not been separately confirmed.
What school does Savannah Village feed into?
Waunakee Community School District. Arboretum Elementary very likely serves the neighborhood — two independent sources, a school-directory site and Premier Builders' own marketing for the Monticello Addition, both associate Savannah Village with Arboretum Elementary. Confirm a specific address with the WCSD Registrar, (608) 849-2000, before writing an offer.
Is there a pool in Savannah Village?
Yes — the Savannah Village Neighborhood Pool, operated by a real, financially active 501(c)(7) nonprofit tax-exempt since 2007. Membership requires a $1,000 one-time initiation fee plus an annual renewal fee (amount stated on the application).
Is there a homeowners association, and what does it cost?
The neighborhood pool has its own well-documented nonprofit association, but a separate covenant-enforcement HOA handling architectural control and general dues could not be identified in this research. Confirm directly with the seller or a title company before assuming either way.
How far is Savannah Village from Madison?
About 24 minutes and 10.2 miles to Capitol Square by routed driving time — noticeably closer than most other Waunakee subdivisions. A commonly repeated marketing claim of "~15 minutes to Madison" does not match this routed figure and shouldn't be relied on.
What parks are near Savannah Village?
Savannah Park is a confirmed, official Village of Waunakee facility, reported to include paved, lighted walking trails and a roller-hockey area — though its exact street address is still being confirmed between two conflicting sources. A separately reported "Hanover Park" with tennis and ball-field facilities has not yet been independently verified.
Is there a solar farm proposed near Savannah Village?
Yes. Six Mile Solar, a proposed 6-megawatt, 30-acre project by OneEnergy Renewables, sits on Kennedy Road directly behind the neighborhood. The Waunakee/Westport Joint Plan Commission denied the project's conditional use permit on February 23, 2026, but the developer filed a Writ of Certiorari in Dane County Circuit Court on March 25, 2026 challenging that denial — the case remains unresolved. This is worth discussing directly with buyers considering the neighborhood's southeast-facing lots.
How many additions make up Savannah Village, and does that matter?
At least five: Montondon, Monticello, Hanover, Eldorado Woods, and Woodbridge. It matters because recorded covenants, dues, and restrictions may differ by addition — a rule confirmed for one should never be assumed to apply to all of them.
