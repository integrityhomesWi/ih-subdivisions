About the Neighborhood

An Established Neighborhood,
Not a New One

Royal Oaks is a classic first-ring Sun Prairie neighborhood — built out mostly in the late 1960s and early 1970s, with some later infill, on the west-central side of the city. Ranch, bi-level, and Colonial Revival homes sit on lots that run up to roughly a third of an acre, larger than what's typical in Sun Prairie's newer west-side subdivisions, under a mature tree canopy that's a genuine differentiator this far into a neighborhood's life.

Royal Oaks is most commonly described with these boundary streets: Windsor Street to the north, Colorado Avenue to the south, Broadway Drive to the east, and Thompson Road to the west. That boundary box comes from a single real-estate source and has not yet been independently checked against a city GIS or plat map, so treat it as a strong lead rather than a confirmed legal boundary, especially at the edges.

"Both an elementary and a middle school sit inside or directly adjacent to Royal Oaks — an unusually convenient setup for families in a Sun Prairie neighborhood this size."

It's also worth being precise about what Royal Oaks is not: available sources describe it as an informal, long-established neighborhood name rather than a developer-branded, HOA-governed subdivision, and no plat date, original developer, or phase history was found. There's no evidence of active new-home construction here — this is a resale-only neighborhood, which some buyers value for exactly that reason: no new-construction premium, and a look and feel that's already settled in.

Schools

Sun Prairie Area School District

Royal Oaks Elementary sits inside the neighborhood and Prairie View Middle School borders it — but high school assignment needs an address-level check before you rely on it.

Elem.

Royal Oaks Elementary

2215 Pennsylvania Ave, Sun Prairie. Named after and physically located in the neighborhood — reasonable to assume in-boundary, but SPASD elementary boundaries aren't always coterminous with neighborhood names, so verify by address. Described in some sources as SPASD's only open-concept school — confirm current building configuration before repeating this, since renovations can change it.

Middle

Prairie View Middle School

400 N. Thompson Road, Sun Prairie. SPASD's own secondary-boundary documentation describes the area "north of Highway 19, west of Broadway Drive, and east of Thompson Road" as Prairie View's attendance area — matching Royal Oaks' east/west boundary streets exactly. A good corroborating signal, but still verify by address.

High

Sun Prairie East or Sun Prairie West — not confirmed

SPASD split into Sun Prairie East (888 Grove Street) and Sun Prairie West (2850 Ironwood Drive) in 2022. Royal Oaks' specific assignment was not confirmed in available sources — do not state a high school for this neighborhood without checking a specific address against SPASD's current boundary tool.

Verify before you write an offer: Royal Oaks' school boundaries — especially the high school split — should be confirmed for the exact address in question at sunprairieschools.org, not assumed from the neighborhood name alone.
