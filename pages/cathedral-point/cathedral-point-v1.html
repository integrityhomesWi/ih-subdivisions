What is Cathedral Point in Verona, Wisconsin?

Cathedral Point is an established residential neighborhood on the southern edge of Verona, Wisconsin, in Dane County, built by Veridian Homes starting in 2009. It centers on Cathedral Point Drive and the landscaped Memory Garden park, with direct Ice Age Trail adjacency. Recent resales (2024–2025) ranged roughly $385,000–$806,100, clustering $500,000–$700,000, per an existing SCWMLS-sourced export — not a live pull. Cathedral Point is zoned for Verona Area School District's Country View Elementary and sits 8 minutes (4.3 miles) from Epic Systems.

About the Neighborhood

Established, Not New Construction.
That's the Point.

Cathedral Point is an established Veridian Homes neighborhood on the southern edge of the City of Verona, Dane County, Wisconsin. The original plat and a First Addition were both recorded in 2009. Today the neighborhood is largely built out and resale-driven — buyers here are shopping the resale market, not a builder's active release schedule.

The neighborhood's spine is Cathedral Point Drive, with interior streets including E. Chapel Royal Drive, Range Trail, Locust Drive, Basilica Parkway, Canterbury Pass, Chad's Crossing, Siena Drive, Whittman Way, Steeple Point Way, and Winchester Pass. E. Whispering Pines Way forms the south edge; Westminster Way forms the north edge. Access is via Highway PB/M from US Hwy 18/151.

"Cathedral Point and The Woods at Cathedral Point share a builder and a border — not a plat, not an HOA, and not the same product."

Cathedral Point is not the same neighborhood as The Woods at Cathedral Point. The Woods is a separate, newer Veridian phase directly across Westminster Way — preliminary plat around 2019, released October 2020 — with its own covenants and its own HOA administration. Original Cathedral Point is the established, resale-only side; The Woods is where the active new-construction buyer choice happens. Some sources place Cathedral Point near the Verona/Fitchburg boundary, since the park's coordinates sit close to that line — the mailing address is Verona, WI 53593.

Veridian Homes, building here since 2009. (A "Forward Development Group" master-developer credit appears in some sources but is not corroborated across all research — treat as unconfirmed.)

Southern edge of the City of Verona, Dane County, off Highway PB/M near US Hwy 18/151. Neighbors include Scenic Ridge, Raywood, Gateway, Kettle Creek, Cross Country, Prairie Oaks, and Swan Creek.

Schools

Verona Area School District

Cathedral Point is zoned for Country View Elementary — the confirmed, current assignment. Older listings and some outdated sources still reference Glacier Edge Elementary, which reflects a prior boundary, not the current one.

Elem.

Country View Elementary School

The confirmed, current elementary assignment for Cathedral Point. If you've seen Glacier Edge Elementary referenced elsewhere for this neighborhood, that reflects an older boundary — Country View is correct as of this page's publish date.

Middle

Badger Ridge Middle School

The confirmed middle school assignment. (Some older sources reference Savanna Oaks — not the current assignment for Cathedral Point.)

High

Verona Area High School

Serves the entire Verona Area School District, grades 9–12.

Boundary note: School attendance boundaries can vary by exact address, especially near a district edge. Country View Elementary and Badger Ridge Middle School are the confirmed current assignments for Cathedral Point — call the Verona Area School District only if you have a specific address concern before writing an offer.

Parks & Recreation

Memory Garden, Trails & Nearby County Parks

The neighborhood's own green space, plus two county parks a short, routed drive away.

🌳

Memory Garden

The landscaped, oval green space at the entrance of Cathedral Point — a real, complete park feature, confirmed against an aerial photo. A separate, older stormwater retention pond exists elsewhere in the area and is sometimes confused with the Memory Garden — they are not the same site.

🛝

Cathedral Point Park

1276 Westminster Way — roughly 5 acres with a playground, basketball court, and shelter, sitting along the boundary with The Woods at Cathedral Point.

Direct trail adjacency, most pronounced for the neighboring Woods at Cathedral Point section. Owners on trail-adjacent lots agree, per HOA covenant, not to oppose controlled burns along the trail edge.

A short, routed drive from Cathedral Point — Dane County parkland with trail access.

Another nearby Dane County park, routed and confirmed at 8 minutes from Cathedral Point.

What's My Home Worth?

Your Home's Value Is Not the Neighborhood Average

Neighborhood market statistics — resale ranges, HOA dues, park proximity — describe the market as a whole. They can't tell you what your specific home is worth today.
