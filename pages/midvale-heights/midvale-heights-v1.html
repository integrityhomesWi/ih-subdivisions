About the Neighborhood

One Name. Five Original Neighborhoods.

Midvale Heights is one of Madison's largest and most established residential neighborhoods, built out primarily from 1947 through 1970 on land that was still open prairie as recently as the 1850s. It didn't start as a single planned development — it absorbed five smaller, separately platted subdivisions under one name: Oakville Heights, Kenmore, Hillview, Third Oak, and Coney Weston.

"I can't think of another place I'd like to live."

— headline of a Wisconsin State Journal (madison.com) feature quoting a Midvale Heights resident. Only the headline and this quote were available for this research pass; the full article wasn't accessible.

Midvale Heights has never had a private developer HOA. Instead, it has been represented since 1954 by the volunteer-run Midvale Heights Community Association — one of Madison's longest-running neighborhood organizations. Whether the association currently collects dues or holds architectural-review authority hasn't been confirmed for this guide; check directly with the association before assuming either way.

Every home here is existing resale stock — there's no new-construction floor plan or builder program in Midvale Heights the way there is in some of the newer subdivisions covered elsewhere on this site. Because most homes were built between 1947 and 1970, most housing stock is 55 to 79 years old as of 2026, which commonly means budgeting for updated electrical, plumbing, roofing, or windows depending on a given home's history — a general pattern for the build era, not a confirmed condition of any specific property.

Origin

Five separately platted 1950s-era neighborhoods — Oakville Heights, Kenmore, Hillview, Third Oak, and Coney Weston — were absorbed under the Midvale Heights name.

Location

Madison's near west side, Dane County. Adjacent to the Westmorland neighborhood; the City of Madison plans the two jointly. Specific boundary streets are not yet confirmed for this guide.

Governance

Midvale Heights Community Association — volunteer-run, founded 1954. No confirmed mandatory HOA or dues.

What is Midvale Heights in Madison, Wisconsin?

Midvale Heights is an established, primarily post-WWII neighborhood on Madison, Wisconsin's near west side, in Dane County. Built out largely from 1947 to 1970, it absorbed five smaller plats — Oakville Heights, Kenmore, Hillview, Third Oak, and Coney Weston — and has been organized since 1954 by the volunteer-run Midvale Heights Community Association. Sale-price data is pending MLS review; contact John Reuter for current figures. It falls within Madison's school jurisdiction (Madison Metropolitan School District, by inference) and borders the Westmorland neighborhood, which the city plans jointly with it.
