What is Kilkenny Farms in Waunakee, Wisconsin?

Kilkenny Farms is an established residential neighborhood in the southern corridor of Waunakee, Wisconsin, in Dane County. Developed starting in 2014 by Livable Communities by Don Tierney on land settled by Irish immigrants in 1848, it is home to the neighborhood's own saltwater pool complex, Kilkenny Farms Park with six pickleball courts, and a trail network along Ireland Drive and Water Wheel Drive. Sold-price data for Kilkenny Farms is pending a fresh SCWMLS pull as of August 2026. Waunakee Community School District serves the neighborhood, which sits directly adjacent to the newer Kilkenny Farms West.

About the Neighborhood

Waunakee's Original Kilkenny.
Built First, Not Named First.

Kilkenny Farms is the neighborhood the "Kilkenny" name started with — developed beginning in 2014 by Livable Communities by Don Tierney on land originally settled by Irish immigrants in 1848, the same heritage that gives the streets here their names. It sits in Waunakee's southern corridor, east of County Road Q and south of Woodland Drive, and today it's largely built out, with resale activity making up most of the current market.

The neighborhood's defining amenity is its own saltwater pool complex — lap pool, activity pool, and kiddie pool — built as part of Kilkenny Farms itself, not borrowed from a neighbor. Kilkenny Farms Park, at 1021 Water Wheel Drive, is one of the better-equipped neighborhood parks in Waunakee: a park shelter, playground with a rope climbing structure and zip line, tennis and basketball courts, and six regulation pickleball courts.

"This is the original Kilkenny — the pool, the park, and the street names all started here before the newer phase next door was ever platted."

A name worth getting right: Kilkenny Farms is a separate, older neighborhood from Kilkenny Farms West, which is a newer, adjacent Waunakee community still under active construction. The two share a name, a developer, a pool amenity, and a general location — which is exactly why buyers, agents, and even MLS records mix them up. If you're comparing the two, see the Kilkenny Farms West guide for the newer phase.

Outdoor Life in Kilkenny Farms

The neighborhood's own park and pool are the headline amenities — with Bolz Conservancy and Governor Nelson State Park within easy reach.

🏓

Kilkenny Farms Park

1021 Water Wheel Drive · 5.42 acres. A park shelter, playground with sand toys, a rope climbing structure and a zip line, sheltered seating, restrooms, tennis and basketball courts on the west side of Water Wheel Drive, and six regulation pickleball courts on the east side. Wheelchair-friendly paths throughout.

🏊

Kilkenny Farms Saltwater Pool Complex

The neighborhood's own pool — lap pool, activity pool, and kiddie pool. Saltwater is gentler on skin and eyes than standard chlorine and is increasingly the standard in premium Waunakee communities. Also used by neighboring Kilkenny Farms West residents.

🚴

Neighborhood Trails

Walking and biking paths run along Ireland Drive and Water Wheel Drive, connecting through the neighborhood toward the Woodland Drive school corridor shared with Kilkenny Farms West.

🌾

Bolz Conservancy — Nearby

Wooded trails opening to hilltop prairie with panoramic views of the Waunakee countryside, adjacent to the Kilkenny corridor. Built-in nature without a long drive.

🏞️

Governor Nelson State Park — Nearby

Lake Mendota access with a sandy beach, swimming, fishing, effigy mounds, and 8+ miles of trails. Waunakee's signature outdoor amenity.

Common Questions

Kilkenny Farms FAQ

Is Kilkenny Farms the same as Kilkenny Farms West?
No — these are two separate Waunakee neighborhoods. Kilkenny Farms is the original neighborhood, developed starting in 2014, and it's home to the shared saltwater pool complex. Kilkenny Farms West is a newer, separate community immediately nearby. Kilkenny Farms West residents have access to the Kilkenny Farms pool, which is a common source of confusion, but the two neighborhoods have different boundaries and different construction timelines.
What schools serve Kilkenny Farms in Waunakee?
Kilkenny Farms is served by the Waunakee Community School District. Based on its Woodland Drive-area location, students are expected to attend Heritage Elementary (K–4), Waunakee Intermediate (5–6), Waunakee Middle School (7–8), and Waunakee Community High School (9–12) — the same schools serving neighboring Kilkenny Farms West. Always verify the exact attendance boundary for a specific address at waunakee.k12.wi.us before writing an offer.
Does Kilkenny Farms have a pool?
Yes. Kilkenny Farms is home to the neighborhood's saltwater pool complex — lap pool, activity pool, and kiddie pool — the same amenity that neighboring Kilkenny Farms West residents also use. Saltwater pools are gentler on skin and eyes than standard chlorine pools.
When was Kilkenny Farms built?
Kilkenny Farms originated in 2014 and was developed by Livable Communities by Don Tierney on land settled by Irish immigrants in 1848. It is largely built out today, with the newer Kilkenny Farms West phase continuing construction immediately adjacent to it.
How far is Kilkenny Farms from downtown Madison?
Kilkenny Farms sits in the same southern Waunakee corridor as neighboring Kilkenny Farms West, roughly 12 miles from downtown Madison. Exact drive times have not yet been independently routed for this guide — treat any specific minutes figure as an estimate until confirmed.
What are the closest dog parks to Kilkenny Farms?
The closest off-leash dog parks are Ripp Park Pet Exercise Area (951 W Main St, Waunakee) and Yahara Heights County Park (28 WI-113, Waunakee). A Dane County Dog Park Permit is required at both.
