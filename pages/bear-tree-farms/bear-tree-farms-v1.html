About the Neighborhood

A DeForest Address, a Windsor Address of Record

Bear Tree Farms is a large, multi-phase, still-actively-developing neighborhood off Windsor Road and Pederson Crossing Boulevard, near the junction of Windsor Road and US Highway 51 — between the villages of Windsor and Sun Prairie. It uses a DeForest, WI 53532 mailing address, and builders and portals market it heavily as "DeForest, WI." But the subdivision is physically platted, permitted, and governed by the Village of Windsor — a different municipality with its own local government, ordinances, and services than DeForest proper. Schools are DeForest Area School District, which is the connective tissue that makes the DeForest branding reasonable even though the municipality is Windsor.

Village of Windsor board resolutions document phased development running from Phase 2 through Phase 8, with recorded actions dated 2020 through 2025 — a reduction in letter of credit for Phase 2 (2020), a Phase 6 developer's agreement (2022), a Phase 7 amendment and water main loop change (2023), and a Phase 8 amendment approved in 2025. As of the most recently confirmed phase, the neighborhood remains under active construction.

"Marketed as DeForest, taxed and governed as Windsor — it's the detail every Bear Tree Farms buyer should hear before they write an offer, not after."

In 2025, Windsor trustees unanimously denied a preliminary replat and rezoning request for a section called "The Highlands" — a proposed senior-housing-oriented change that also sought to vacate portions of Honeycomb Lane, Honey Bee Court, Eagle Mound Pass, Ramshorn Drive, and Grouse Woods Road. Existing residents opposed the change at a public hearing, saying they'd bought based on the original plans. It's a real, documented local matter worth knowing about before engaging owners in that section — and, taken neutrally, a sign of an engaged resident base. Development plans also describe a small commercial node within or adjacent to the neighborhood: a Door Creek Church campus, the Yahara River Learning Center, and Paradise Paws Doggie Day Care. Cortland Park (a smaller condo/duplex development at the SW corner of Pederson Crossing Blvd and Reardon Road) and Mt. Warner (a small single-family plat rezoned to be consistent with Bear Tree Farms) sit adjacent to, but are not part of, Bear Tree Farms.

Common Questions

Bear Tree Farms FAQ

Is Bear Tree Farms actually in DeForest, Wisconsin?
Its mailing address is DeForest, WI 53532, and builders and real estate portals market it as a DeForest neighborhood — but Bear Tree Farms is physically located in, and governed by, the Village of Windsor, a different municipality with its own local government and ordinances. Schools fall under the DeForest Area School District, which is likely why the DeForest branding persists. Buyers should understand they are purchasing in Windsor, not DeForest proper.
What school district serves Bear Tree Farms?
Bear Tree Farms is served by the DeForest Area School District. Windsor Elementary School (4352 Windsor Road, Windsor, WI 53598) is the confirmed elementary building; DeForest Middle School and DeForest Area High School are widely cited for middle and high school by multiple sources but not independently confirmed against a district boundary map. Because DeForest Area SD operates multiple elementary buildings and attendance boundaries can shift, confirm the exact school assignment for any specific address with the district before writing an offer.
Is there a homeowners association at Bear Tree Farms?
Yes — the Bear Tree Farms Homeowners Association (beartreefarmshoa.com). The current annual due amount has not been independently confirmed in our research and can vary by phase in a phased development like this one. Contact the HOA directly or ask John for current, confirmed figures before assuming a fee.
Is Bear Tree Farms still under construction?
Yes. Village of Windsor board records document development phases continuing at least through Phase 8, approved in 2025, so parts of the neighborhood remain active construction sites. Residents in earlier phases may be living near ongoing building activity for the foreseeable future.
What amenities does Bear Tree Farms have?
The centerpiece is a roughly 22-acre community park with a splash pad, a combination tennis/basketball court, a pickleball court, multiple playgrounds, a bicycle traffic course, and public restrooms, plus a trail network through the neighborhood. Some marketing materials have referenced park elements as renderings at earlier points, so confirm the current built-out status of any specific amenity before relying on it.
Who builds in Bear Tree Farms?
Multiple builders are active, including Tim O'Brien Homes, Eldon Homes, and custom builds facilitated through Design Custom Homes of Madison. Because several builders operate across different phases, home style and price point can vary meaningfully depending on which section you're looking at.
Can I choose any builder for my lot in Bear Tree Farms?
Not confidently confirmed. One marketing source describes broad builder choice, but this has not been verified across every phase — some sections may be builder-exclusive. Confirm current builder restrictions for any specific lot before assuming you have free choice.

Windsor Elementary School (4352 Windsor Road, Windsor, WI 53598) is the confirmed elementary building; DeForest Middle School and DeForest Area High School are widely cited for middle and high school by multiple sources but not independently confirmed against a district boundary map. Because DeForest Area SD operates multiple elementary buildings and attendance boundaries can shift, confirm the exact school assignment for any specific address with the district before writing an offer.
Is there a homeowners association at Bear Tree Farms?
Yes — the Bear Tree Farms Homeowners Association (beartreefarmshoa.com). The current annual due amount has not been independently confirmed in our research and can vary by phase in a phased development like this one. Contact the HOA directly or ask John for current, confirmed figures before assuming a fee.
Is Bear Tree Farms still under construction?
Yes. Village of Windsor board records document development phases continuing at least through Phase 8, approved in 2025, so parts of the neighborhood remain active construction sites. Residents in earlier phases may be living near ongoing building activity for the foreseeable future.
What amenities does Bear Tree Farms have?
The centerpiece is a roughly 22-acre community park with a splash pad, a combination tennis/basketball court, a pickleball court, multiple playgrounds, a bicycle traffic course, and public restrooms, plus a trail network through the neighborhood. Some marketing materials have referenced park elements as renderings at earlier points, so confirm the current built-out status of any specific amenity before relying on it.
Who builds in Bear Tree Farms?
Multiple builders are active, including Tim O'Brien Homes, Eldon Homes, and custom builds facilitated through Design Custom Homes of Madison. Because several builders operate across different phases, home style and price point can vary meaningfully depending on which section you're looking at.
Can I choose any builder for my lot in Bear Tree Farms?
Not confidently confirmed. One marketing source describes broad builder choice, but this has not been verified across every phase — some sections may be builder-exclusive. Confirm current builder restrictions for any specific lot before assuming you have free choice.
