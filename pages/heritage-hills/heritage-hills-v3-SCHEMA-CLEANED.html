🏡 About Heritage Hills ›
About the Neighborhood

A Farm Family's Land,
Now a Growing Community

Heritage Hills sits on 160 acres on the east side of Waunakee that the Breunig family farmed for more than 70 years before selling to Veridian Homes — reportedly on the condition that the development include an affordable-housing component. Streets like Breunig Boulevard carry the family's name forward. Village review began in early 2020, with the first phase (79 homes plus a pocket park) approved that year and a groundbreaking ceremony held in 2021. The project is planned as a long, 10-to-12-year buildout: an estimated 478 single-family and Haven twin homes, a separately managed 200-unit apartment community, roughly 20 acres of dedicated park space, and a small mixed-use commercial node near the entrance.

Construction has continued in phases since: Village planning records show Phase 3 released in September 2022, a Phase 7 submittal reviewed in March 2024, Phase 8 (18 lots) released for sale in September 2025, and Phase 9 (46 additional homesites — 38 single-family, 8 Haven twin homes) receiving final-plat approval in 2026, with a builder price release expected August 20, 2026. Municipal engineering records already reference work for a Phase 10, so the buildout is expected to continue for several more years.

The neighborhood sits off North Division Street near East Main Street (Highway 19/113), directly adjacent to Dane County's Schumacher Farm County Park — meaning established trails and green space sit right at the development's edge, not something the developer had to build from scratch.

"A farm that fed a family for 70 years is becoming a neighborhood with room for thousands — with the county park it always bordered still right next door."

Because this is an active, multi-phase build, expect construction, evolving traffic patterns, and amenities that are still being completed in some sections. One fact every buyer should know going in: an active quarry operates near the development, and blasting tied to rock extraction has produced documented resident concerns going back to a February 2021 Village Board hearing, with blasting notices continuing into 2026 as later phases are built out — see the info box below and the FAQ for details.

Know before you go: Heritage Hills' recorded covenants disclose an active quarry near the subdivision. A Village Board public hearing on February 16, 2021 documented resident complaints of blasting-related foundation cracking and house-shaking, and Village of Waunakee blasting notices have continued into 2026 tied to ongoing construction (most recently for Phase 9, in late July and early August 2026). Ask your agent for the current blasting schedule and status before writing an offer — see the FAQ below.

Builder

Veridian Homes — single-family and "Haven" maintenance-included twin homes.

Location

East side of Waunakee, off N. Division St near E. Main St (Hwy 19/113), bordering Schumacher Farm County Park.

Scale

160 acres · ~478 planned single-family/twin homes · 200-unit apartment community (built 2023, separate from the HOA) · ~20 acres of planned park space.

Current Phase

Phase 8 (18 lots) is the active for-sale phase. Phase 9 (46 lots) has final-plat approval, with builder pricing expected August 20, 2026.

HOA

Heritage Hills Waunakee Homeowners Association, Inc., managed by DSI Real Estate Group. 2026 base dues: $240.65/home/year. Haven homes pay that plus a separate monthly maintenance fee (exact amount not publicly posted — confirm directly). Board remains developer-controlled as of the most recent annual meeting.

Builder-Quoted Pricing

Haven twin homes from ~$439,900 (The Piper) up to ~$524,900 (The Julian); single-family from ~$440,000. Builder pricing moves — confirm current pricing with John or Veridian directly.

What Draws Buyers to Heritage Hills

A newer, larger-scale community with a housing mix and county-park access that's different from Waunakee's smaller, finished subdivisions.

⭐ Why People Choose It ▾
01

Schumacher Farm Park Next Door

Direct adjacency to an established Dane County park with trails and open green space — access that predates the subdivision itself, not a developer amenity still being built.

02

"Haven" Maintenance-Included Twin Homes

Twin-home living with lawn mowing, fertilization, snow removal, and seasonal maintenance handled through a separate sub-association fee — a genuine draw for buyers who want new construction without the upkeep.

03

A Range of Housing in One Community

Single-family homes, Haven twin homes, and a separate 200-unit apartment community all sit within the same master plan — a wider range of price points and life stages than many single-product Waunakee subdivisions.

04

New Construction from an Established Builder

Veridian Homes has a long track record in the greater Madison market, with current floor plans and builder warranties across both single-family and twin-home product.

05

Close to Downtown Waunakee

About a 4-minute drive or roughly a mile to downtown Waunakee's shops, restaurants, and the public library — confirmed with real routing data from 632 Hillcrest Drive.

06

A Story Behind the Streets

The Breunig family farmed this land for more than 70 years before selling it for development — their name lives on in Breunig Boulevard, and their sale reportedly came with a condition to include affordable housing.

07

Architectural Standards Built In

The HOA's Architectural Control Committee reviews exterior materials and design — encouraged styles include Cottage, Prairie, Craftsman, Farmhouse, and Traditional, with a consistent roofing standard across the neighborhood.

08

Still Actively Building

With Phase 9's 46 lots approved for 2026 and Phase 10 already referenced in engineering records, buyers today can still choose from newer sections as the community continues to develop.

Heritage Hills FAQ

❓ FAQ ▾
Who is developing Heritage Hills in Waunakee?
Veridian Homes, on a 160-acre former Breunig family farm on the east side of Waunakee. Village review began in early 2020, and the project is planned as a 10-to-12-year buildout. As of August 2026, Phase 8 is the active for-sale phase and Phase 9 (46 additional homesites) has final-plat approval, with builder pricing expected August 20, 2026.
Is there really a quarry near Heritage Hills?
Yes. The recorded covenants disclose an active quarry operation near the subdivision. A Village Board public hearing on February 16, 2021 covered resident complaints about blasting-related foundation cracking and house-shaking, and Village of Waunakee blasting notices have continued into 2026 tied to ongoing construction phases — most recently for Phase 9, in late July and early August 2026. Ask your agent for the current blasting schedule before writing an offer.
What types of homes are in Heritage Hills?
Traditional single-family homes and twin homes marketed as "Haven" maintenance-included homes (lawn care and snow removal handled through a separate fee), plus a separately managed 200-unit apartment community — Heritage Hills Apartments, 110 Breunig Blvd — confirmed not to be part of the homeowners association.
Is there an HOA, and what does it cost?
Yes — Heritage Hills Waunakee Homeowners Association, Inc., managed by DSI Real Estate Group. The 2026 base assessment is $240.65 per home per year. Haven twin-home owners pay that base amount plus a separate monthly maintenance fee whose exact dollar figure isn't publicly published — confirm it directly with the builder or HOA management. The board remains developer-controlled as of the most recent annual meeting.
What school district serves Heritage Hills?
Waunakee Community School District. Heritage Elementary very likely serves the subdivision based on district transportation policy, but the district's own interactive boundary tool couldn't be checked address by address for this guide. Confirm your specific address with the WCSD Registrar, (608) 849-2000 ext. 8470, before writing an offer.
How close is Heritage Hills to downtown Waunakee?
About a 4-minute drive or roughly 1 mile, confirmed with routing data from 632 Hillcrest Drive as of August 2026. It's roughly a 21-minute walk or 8-minute bike ride.
Are the neighborhood's parks and dog park finished?
Not entirely, as of mid-2026. A neighborhood park with a pavilion appears to be built per the builder's own materials, but the planned roughly 2.7-acre dog park was still an open discussion item on a Village Parks Committee agenda as of June 23, 2026. Confirm current status before assuming everything is complete.
What are homes selling for in Heritage Hills?

Is the apartment community part of the Heritage Hills HOA?
No. Heritage Hills Apartments (110 Breunig Blvd, 200 units, built 2023) is professionally managed separately and is confirmed not to be governed by the Heritage Hills Waunakee Homeowners Association, even though the same management company is involved with both.

What is Heritage Hills in Waunakee, Wisconsin?

Heritage Hills is a newer, actively-building residential neighborhood on the east side of Waunakee, Wisconsin, in Dane County. Developed by Veridian Homes since 2021 on a 160-acre former Breunig family farm, it includes single-family homes, maintenance-included "Haven" twin homes, and a neighborhood park with a pavilion, adjacent to Schumacher Farm County Park. Common Questions

Heritage Hills FAQ

❓ FAQ ▾
Who is developing Heritage Hills in Waunakee?
Veridian Homes, on a 160-acre former Breunig family farm on the east side of Waunakee. Village review began in early 2020, and the project is planned as a 10-to-12-year buildout. As of August 2026, Phase 8 is the active for-sale phase and Phase 9 (46 additional homesites) has final-plat approval, with builder pricing expected August 20, 2026.
Is there really a quarry near Heritage Hills?
Yes. The recorded covenants disclose an active quarry operation near the subdivision. A Village Board public hearing on February 16, 2021 covered resident complaints about blasting-related foundation cracking and house-shaking, and Village of Waunakee blasting notices have continued into 2026 tied to ongoing construction phases — most recently for Phase 9, in late July and early August 2026. Ask your agent for the current blasting schedule before writing an offer.
What types of homes are in Heritage Hills?
Traditional single-family homes and twin homes marketed as "Haven" maintenance-included homes (lawn care and snow removal handled through a separate fee), plus a separately managed 200-unit apartment community — Heritage Hills Apartments, 110 Breunig Blvd — confirmed not to be part of the homeowners association.
Is there an HOA, and what does it cost?
Yes — Heritage Hills Waunakee Homeowners Association, Inc., managed by DSI Real Estate Group. The 2026 base assessment is $240.65 per home per year. Haven twin-home owners pay that base amount plus a separate monthly maintenance fee whose exact dollar figure isn't publicly published — confirm it directly with the builder or HOA management. The board remains developer-controlled as of the most recent annual meeting.
What school district serves Heritage Hills?
Waunakee Community School District. Heritage Elementary very likely serves the subdivision based on district transportation policy, but the district's own interactive boundary tool couldn't be checked address by address for this guide. Confirm your specific address with the WCSD Registrar, (608) 849-2000 ext. 8470, before writing an offer.
How close is Heritage Hills to downtown Waunakee?
About a 4-minute drive or roughly 1 mile, confirmed with routing data from 632 Hillcrest Drive as of August 2026. It's roughly a 21-minute walk or 8-minute bike ride.
Are the neighborhood's parks and dog park finished?
Not entirely, as of mid-2026. A neighborhood park with a pavilion appears to be built per the builder's own materials, but the planned roughly 2.7-acre dog park was still an open discussion item on a Village Parks Committee agenda as of June 23, 2026. Confirm current status before assuming everything is complete.
What are homes selling for in Heritage Hills?
Contact John Reuter at 608-669-4226 for current SCWMLS subdivision-specific sold data and a market analysis on any specific home — this figure should never be sourced from Zillow, Redfin, or similar third-party estimate sites.
Is the apartment community part of the Heritage Hills HOA?
No. Heritage Hills Apartments (110 Breunig Blvd, 200 units, built 2023) is professionally managed separately and is confirmed not to be governed by the Heritage Hills Waunakee Homeowners Association, even though the same management company is involved with both.
