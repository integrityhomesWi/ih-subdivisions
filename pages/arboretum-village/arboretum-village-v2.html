About the Neighborhood

An Active, Still-Building Neighborhood
With Three Housing Products

Arboretum Village is an active, mixed-product residential development on the southeastern side of the Village of Waunakee, off Hogan Road, developed by Livable Communities by Don Tierney — the same office behind Westbridge, Golden Ponds, Savannah Village, and Southbridge elsewhere in this research. Unlike several other, largely built-out Waunakee neighborhoods, Arboretum Village is genuinely active today: Tim O'Brien Homes currently markets 14-plus floor plans and buildable homesites here.

The Village's approved General Development Plan covers three distinct residential products — 102 detached single-family lots, seven condominium lots (planned for two dwellings each), and one multifamily parcel (Lot 61), later developed into ten duplex units on a shared driveway. These are three separate housing categories with their own likely dues, maintenance, and governance structures — never assumed to be interchangeable. A genuine, unresolved discrepancy exists over the final condominium unit count (14 per the approved plan vs. 20 per a separate source).

The neighborhood includes a genuinely well-documented park: Laubmeier Park, dedicated in September 2022 and named for John Laubmeier, a 16-year Village President and longtime educator, with an adjacent reported nine-acre wooded trail area called Teddy Bear Woods. The development also sits directly adjacent to the Waunakee Business Park, which is why the Village required covenant disclosure language about potential noise, vibration, odor, and night-operations impacts for nearby lots.

"Arboretum Village isn't a detached-home-only neighborhood — it's genuinely three housing products under one name, still actively building today."

Know before you go: Arboretum Village directly adjoins the Waunakee Business Park near Highway 19 and Hogan Road. Village planning records required covenant disclosure language addressing potential noise, vibration, odors, night operations, and outdoor storage for lots near that boundary — a real, substantive consideration for any buyer looking at a lot near the edge of the neighborhood, not generic boilerplate.

Is Arboretum Village still being actively built?
Yes. Tim O'Brien Homes is an active current builder marketing 14-plus floor plans as of this research, alongside buildable homesites — a meaningful difference from several other, largely built-out Waunakee subdivisions covered in this research.
Who developed Arboretum Village?

Is there a homeowners association, and what does it cost?
An HOA almost certainly exists — the Village's own approval record recommends architectural authority rest with an association rather than the Village, and the HOA is specifically responsible for maintaining an on-site historic grave marker — but this research could not confirm the association's legal name, dues, or specific architectural-control rules. What park amenities does Arboretum Village have?

Are there condos in Arboretum Village, and are they the same as the detached homes?
No — they're a separate housing product. Arboretum Village includes a planned condominium association (reported as either 14 or 20 units, depending on the source — a discrepancy not yet resolved) on Irene Court, in addition to the detached single-family homes and a smaller duplex component (Lot 61, 10 units).
Is Arboretum Village near any commercial or industrial development?
Yes — it's directly adjacent to the Waunakee Business Park (Hwy 19 & Hogan Rd), which is why the Village required covenant disclosure language about potential noise, vibration, odor, and night-operations impacts for nearby lots.
How far is Arboretum Village from downtown Waunakee?
About 6 minutes and 2.7 miles by routed driving time from Laubmeier Park (1155 Prairie View Drive).
Is Arboretum Village part of Savannah Village?
No. The two developments are adjacent and connected by street access (Thistle Dew Lane routes toward Savannah Village), but they are separately platted, with their own additions and covenant structures.

Common Questions

Arboretum Village FAQ

✍️ FAQ ▾
Is Arboretum Village still being actively built?
Yes. Tim O'Brien Homes is an active current builder marketing 14-plus floor plans as of this research, alongside buildable homesites — a meaningful difference from several other, largely built-out Waunakee subdivisions covered in this research.
Who developed Arboretum Village?
Livable Communities by Don Tierney, with Tim O'Brien Homes as a current active builder. The same Tierney office is behind Westbridge, Golden Ponds, Savannah Village, and Southbridge elsewhere in Waunakee.
What school does Arboretum Village feed into?
Waunakee Community School District. Arboretum Elementary is very likely — the routed driving distance is under a mile (about 3 minutes), and an independent real-estate source separately describes the neighborhood as within walking distance of the school. Confirm a specific address with the WCSD Registrar, (608) 849-2000, before writing an offer.
Is there a homeowners association, and what does it cost?
An HOA almost certainly exists — the Village's own approval record recommends architectural authority rest with an association rather than the Village, and the HOA is specifically responsible for maintaining an on-site historic grave marker — but this research could not confirm the association's legal name, dues, or specific architectural-control rules. Confirm directly with the developer or a title company before closing.
What park amenities does Arboretum Village have?
Laubmeier Park (1155 Prairie View Drive), dedicated in September 2022 and named for John Laubmeier, a 16-year Village President and longtime educator — featuring an open-air shelter, a pickleball court, and a playground, plus an adjacent reported nine-acre wooded trail area known as Teddy Bear Woods.
Are there condos in Arboretum Village, and are they the same as the detached homes?
No — they're a separate housing product. Arboretum Village includes a planned condominium association (reported as either 14 or 20 units, depending on the source — a discrepancy not yet resolved) on Irene Court, in addition to the detached single-family homes and a smaller duplex component (Lot 61, 10 units).
Is Arboretum Village near any commercial or industrial development?
Yes — it's directly adjacent to the Waunakee Business Park (Hwy 19 & Hogan Rd), which is why the Village required covenant disclosure language about potential noise, vibration, odor, and night-operations impacts for nearby lots.
How far is Arboretum Village from downtown Waunakee?
About 6 minutes and 2.7 miles by routed driving time from Laubmeier Park (1155 Prairie View Drive).
Is Arboretum Village part of Savannah Village?
No. The two developments are adjacent and connected by street access (Thistle Dew Lane routes toward Savannah Village), but they are separately platted, with their own additions and covenant structures.
What are homes selling for in Arboretum Village?
Homes in Arboretum Village sold between $597,000 and $1,647,500 over the trailing 12 months, with a median of $892,250, per an MLS export pulled by John Reuter in August 2026 — a wide range that blends detached homes, the Irene Court condos, and the Lot 61 duplex units. Builder-advertised prices for current new-construction floor plans range roughly $599,900–$1,399,900 (Tim O'Brien Homes) as of this research — that's builder pricing, not a market/resale indicator.
