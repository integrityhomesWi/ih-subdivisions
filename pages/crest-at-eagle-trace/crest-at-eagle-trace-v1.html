About the Neighborhood

Veridian's Newest Middleton
Neighborhood, Verified Carefully

Crest at Eagle Trace is an active, currently-selling new-construction neighborhood built by Veridian Homes on Middleton's far west side, off the Old Sauk Road / Schewe Road corridor near Pope Farm Conservancy. It is marketed as the "Crest Collection" — a distinct, more attainably priced product line of twin homes and simplified single-family plans, built within or immediately adjacent to Veridian's larger Eagle Trace master community. A Veridian social post captured in search results describes it as "our newest Middleton neighborhood," suggesting Crest is the newer of the two related brands.

Marketing copy references three heirloom oak trees planted by three Schewe sisters as a heritage nod to the land's farming history — a checkable, name-tied detail rather than generic landscaping language. The exact plat boundary between "Eagle Trace" and "Crest at Eagle Trace" has not been independently confirmed; the one street verified to sit specifically inside Crest at Eagle Trace is Mosaic Way.

"Get into this Pope Farm Conservancy-adjacent pocket of Middleton starting in the $400s" is the pitch — but that price point needs a live confirmation call to Veridian before it goes in front of a client.

This page is built entirely from a single-source AI research pass (the Perplexity leg of the standard three-engine research process did not complete for this subdivision). Several standard facts — HOA status, exact plat phase count, boundary-assigned schools, and a resolved price range — remain open items for John to verify directly with Veridian Homes and MCPASD before this page is treated as fully settled.

Middleton's far west side, off the Old Sauk Road / Schewe Road corridor, near Pope Farm Conservancy, roughly a few miles west of the Beltline (US-12/18).
