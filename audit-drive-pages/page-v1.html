🏡 About Golden Ponds ›
About the Neighborhood

A Newer Addition, Built
on Its Own Recorded Rules

Golden Ponds — formally the Golden Ponds Addition to Westbridge — is a newer-construction subdivision on the northwest side of the Village of Waunakee, developed by Westbridge, LLC and marketed through Livable Communities by Don Tierney. It sits north of Kopp Road, between Hellenbrand Road and an active railroad line, immediately adjacent to the older Westbridge subdivision built by the same development team. Although the two neighborhoods share street connections and a developer relationship, Golden Ponds has its own recorded declaration, its own homeowners association, and its own development timeline — it should be treated as its own subdivision, not a subsection of original Westbridge.

The Village of Waunakee's Plan Commission recommended approval on May 13, 2019, and the Village Board approved the final plat and developer's agreement on May 20, 2019, when the project was described as roughly 62 acres and 83 residential units. The governing Declaration of Covenants, Restrictions, Conditions and Easements was recorded with Dane County on October 4, 2022, as Document #5865853, identifying the final Lots 1–80 plus Outlots 1–8 — a modest refinement between planning and recording, not a contradiction. Livable Communities announced homesites publicly available beginning spring 2023, and the neighborhood was featured in the 2024 Madison Area Parade of Homes, with construction continuing into 2026. Two lot combinations have since been recorded: Nancy Court Lots 24–25 (retired July 17, 2024) and Roger Court Lots 36–37 (Village Board approved February 19, 2025; retired March 4, 2025).

Golden Ponds also carries substantial engineered drainage and environmental infrastructure. Vierbicher's civil-engineering work for the project relocated former agricultural drainage ditches, created wetland-mitigation areas, and built stormwater-treatment ponds intended to reduce nutrient movement toward Lake Mendota — meaning the neighborhood's ponds and drainage corridors are functional infrastructure, not simply decorative water features.

"Golden Ponds shares a developer and several street names with original Westbridge next door — but it has its own recorded declaration, its own HOA, and its own governance. The two should never be treated as one subdivision."

The recorded declaration sets Golden Ponds apart with binding design requirements: detached single-family homes only, minimum floor areas (1,600 sq ft single-story, 2,000 sq ft two-story, 1,600 sq ft main-level for raised ranch/bi-level/tri-level homes), a required attached 2–3 stall garage with detached garages prohibited, a generally required 8/12 front roof pitch (with a Prairie-style exception), required brick or stone veneer in specified foundation applications, and prohibitions on above-ground pools, chain-link/vinyl/plastic fencing, and short-term rentals. Real built examples — including a 3,026-square-foot Bill Weber Jr. Homes listing at 906 Travis Larson Court and a 4,284-square-foot 2024 Parade of Homes entry — confirm homes are being built well above the covenant minimums, with 3-stall attached garages and finished walkout lower levels where the lot allows.

The Golden Ponds Addition to Westbridge Homeowners Association, Inc. (WI DFI Entity ID G067822) registered October 3, 2022 and remains, per the most recent available filing (a registered-agent renewal dated November 1, 2025), governed by the developer — Donald C. Tierney's own DeForest, WI address remains the registered agent and principal office, with no filing yet showing a transition to an owner-elected board or the declaration's three-person Design Review Committee.

Know before you go: Golden Ponds' recorded declaration discloses that the addition's eastern boundary adjoins an active railroad line, with potential noise, vibration, exhaust, and night-operations effects, and it also carries a recorded Right-to-Farm disclosure covering nearby agricultural operations. Both are legitimate, recorded disclosures rather than speculation — worth reviewing directly before assuming them away.

Developer

Westbridge, LLC (developer/declarant), marketed through Livable Communities by Don Tierney — also behind original Westbridge, Southbridge, Savannah Village, and Kilkenny Farms/Kilkenny Farms West, all separate, non-adjacent Waunakee developments.

Location

North of Kopp Road, between Hellenbrand Road and an active railroad line, on Waunakee's northwest side — immediately adjacent to, but legally separate from, the original Westbridge plat.

Recorded Declaration

October 4, 2022 (Dane County Doc #5865853). Lots 1–80 plus Outlots 1–8. Village Board final-plat approval came earlier, May 20, 2019.

HOA

Golden Ponds Addition to Westbridge Homeowners Association, Inc. (WI DFI ID G067822). Developer-controlled as of the most recent (Nov. 2025) filing — separate from original Westbridge's own association.

Housing

Detached single-family only, with recorded minimum sizes and design standards. Active builders include Bill Weber Jr. Homes, Coogan Builders, and Classic Custom Homes of Waunakee. One builder-advertised 2026 listing: $924,900 (not resale data).

Newer Construction, Binding Design Standards

Village-Owned Park Land

Engineered Stormwater & Wetland Corridor

Outlots 1, 5, and 8 replaced a former agricultural drainage ditch with an engineered stream and wetland-mitigation system, reducing nutrient runoff toward Lake Mendota.

Optional Neighborhood Pool Access

Golden Ponds residents may join the separately run Westbridge Neighborhood Pool on a paid membership basis, without that cost folded into HOA dues.

Active, Ongoing New-Home Construction

Multiple builders — Bill Weber Jr. Homes, Coogan Builders, Classic Custom Homes of Waunakee, and others — remain engaged, including 2024–2026 Parade of Homes participation.

06

Genuine In-Village Proximity

Real routed distances from a Golden Ponds address put grocery, coffee, pharmacy, and downtown Waunakee within about two miles and five minutes.

07

Waunakee School District Assignment

Confirmed via Dane County's own property records — for buyers specifically seeking Waunakee Community School District.

08

Adjacent to an Established Neighborhood

Golden Ponds sits directly next to the older, largely built-out original Westbridge plat — established streets and an existing, golf-course-adjacent neighborhood sit right next door, even though Golden Ponds is governed separately.

Golden Ponds Park — Outlots 2, 3 & 6 Developer-Marketed · Village Confirmation Needed

The developer markets an on-site park with an open-air shelter, playground, and pickleball court on Village-owned park outlots (906 Welcome Blvd, 920 Travis Larson Ct, and 909 Welcome Blvd). No independent Village Parks & Recreation source confirms current completion — a recent Waunakee Tribune roundup of the Village's active 2025 park priorities didn't mention Golden Ponds, which could mean the park was already finished and non-newsworthy, or simply wasn't a funded priority that year.

💧

Stormwater Outlots — Outlots 1, 5 & 8 Verified Infrastructure

Engineered stormwater-management ponds and a meandering stream/wetland-mitigation corridor that replaced a former agricultural drainage ditch, designed by Vierbicher to reduce nutrient loading toward Lake Mendota. This is functioning infrastructure, not decorative or confirmed-recreational green space.

📬

Mail-Kiosk Outlots — Outlots 4 & 7 Field Verification Needed

Association-related outlots tied to neighborhood mail kiosks. Current parking, snow-clearing, and access conditions have not been field-verified.

🏊

Westbridge Neighborhood Pool (Optional, Separate Membership)

Golden Ponds residents are eligible for optional, separately paid membership in the Westbridge Neighborhood Pool (saltwater activity pool, lap pool, zero-depth children's pool), run by a distinct nonprofit — not automatically bundled into HOA dues. Current membership pricing was not found this research pass.

⛳

Meadows of Sixmile Creek Golf Course

In the broader area, but not directly adjacent to Golden Ponds the way it is to original Westbridge next door. No evidence of any Golden-Ponds-specific membership, playing privileges, or discount.

Is Golden Ponds the same as Westbridge?
No. Golden Ponds Addition to Westbridge is a newer, legally distinct subdivision built by the same development team immediately adjacent to the older Westbridge plat. It has its own recorded declaration (2022) and its own separate homeowners association — the two should never be treated as one subdivision or one HOA.
Who developed Golden Ponds?
Westbridge, LLC is the developer/declarant of record, with the neighborhood marketed through Livable Communities by Don Tierney — the same team behind original Westbridge, Southbridge, Savannah Village, Kilkenny Farms, and Kilkenny Farms West elsewhere in Waunakee, all separate, non-adjacent developments.
When was Golden Ponds approved and recorded?
The Village Board approved the final plat and developer's agreement on May 20, 2019, when the project was described as roughly 83 units on 62 acres. The governing declaration was recorded with Dane County on October 4, 2022 (Document #5865853), identifying the final Lots 1–80 plus 8 outlots — a refinement between planning and recording, not a conflict.
Does Golden Ponds have its own HOA, and is it developer- or resident-controlled?
Yes — the Golden Ponds Addition to Westbridge Homeowners Association, Inc., a separate legal entity from the original Westbridge Neighborhood Association. As of the most recent available Wisconsin DFI filing (a registered-agent renewal dated November 1, 2025), it remains developer-controlled, with no filing yet showing a transition to an owner-elected board or the declaration's Design Review Committee.
How much are Golden Ponds' HOA dues?
Not fully confirmed for 2026. The declaration set a maximum annual assessment of $250 per lot at the time of recording in 2022, subject to increases tied to actual association costs — that's a 2022 recording-time ceiling, not a confirmed current figure. Confirm current dues directly with the HOA or developer's office before budgeting for a purchase.
What design restrictions come with a Golden Ponds home?
The recorded declaration requires detached single-family homes only, minimum floor areas (1,600 SF single-story, 2,000 SF two-story, 1,600 SF main-level for raised ranch/bi-level/tri-level), an attached 2–3 stall garage (detached garages prohibited), generally an 8/12 front roof pitch with a Prairie-style exception, brick/stone veneer in specified applications, and prohibits above-ground pools and chain-link/vinyl/plastic fencing. Confirm the full covenant text directly before finalizing plans.
Are short-term rentals allowed in Golden Ponds?
No — the recorded declaration expressly prohibits short-term or transient rentals of homes, rooms, or bedrooms, subject to a limited family-related exception.
Does Golden Ponds have a park?
Village-owned park outlots (Outlots 2, 3, and 6) are confirmed transferred to the Village. The developer markets a shelter, playground, and pickleball court on these outlots, but current completion has not been independently confirmed by a Village source — treat it as developer-claimed pending field or Village confirmation.
Is there pool access at Golden Ponds?
Yes, on an optional, separately paid membership basis in the Westbridge Neighborhood Pool, run by a distinct nonprofit — not automatically bundled into HOA dues. Current membership pricing was not found this research pass.

Confirm your exact address with the WCSD Registrar, (608) 849-2000, before writing an offer.
Is there a railroad near Golden Ponds?
Yes — Golden Ponds' eastern boundary adjoins an active railroad line, and the recorded declaration includes a disclosure of potential noise, vibration, exhaust, and night-operations effects, alongside a separate Right-to-Farm disclosure for nearby agricultural land.
Is Golden Ponds still building new homes, and what are homes selling for?

Common Questions

Golden Ponds FAQ

✍️ FAQ ▾
Is Golden Ponds the same as Westbridge?
No. Golden Ponds Addition to Westbridge is a newer, legally distinct subdivision built by the same development team immediately adjacent to the older Westbridge plat. It has its own recorded declaration (2022) and its own separate homeowners association — the two should never be treated as one subdivision or one HOA.
Who developed Golden Ponds?
Westbridge, LLC is the developer/declarant of record, with the neighborhood marketed through Livable Communities by Don Tierney — the same team behind original Westbridge, Southbridge, Savannah Village, Kilkenny Farms, and Kilkenny Farms West elsewhere in Waunakee, all separate, non-adjacent developments.
When was Golden Ponds approved and recorded?
The Village Board approved the final plat and developer's agreement on May 20, 2019, when the project was described as roughly 83 units on 62 acres. The governing declaration was recorded with Dane County on October 4, 2022 (Document #5865853), identifying the final Lots 1–80 plus 8 outlots — a refinement between planning and recording, not a conflict.
Does Golden Ponds have its own HOA, and is it developer- or resident-controlled?
Yes — the Golden Ponds Addition to Westbridge Homeowners Association, Inc., a separate legal entity from the original Westbridge Neighborhood Association. As of the most recent available Wisconsin DFI filing (a registered-agent renewal dated November 1, 2025), it remains developer-controlled, with no filing yet showing a transition to an owner-elected board or the declaration's Design Review Committee.
How much are Golden Ponds' HOA dues?
Not fully confirmed for 2026. The declaration set a maximum annual assessment of $250 per lot at the time of recording in 2022, subject to increases tied to actual association costs — that's a 2022 recording-time ceiling, not a confirmed current figure. Confirm current dues directly with the HOA or developer's office before budgeting for a purchase.
What design restrictions come with a Golden Ponds home?
The recorded declaration requires detached single-family homes only, minimum floor areas (1,600 SF single-story, 2,000 SF two-story, 1,600 SF main-level for raised ranch/bi-level/tri-level), an attached 2–3 stall garage (detached garages prohibited), generally an 8/12 front roof pitch with a Prairie-style exception, brick/stone veneer in specified applications, and prohibits above-ground pools and chain-link/vinyl/plastic fencing. Confirm the full covenant text directly before finalizing plans.
Are short-term rentals allowed in Golden Ponds?
No — the recorded declaration expressly prohibits short-term or transient rentals of homes, rooms, or bedrooms, subject to a limited family-related exception.
Does Golden Ponds have a park?
Village-owned park outlots (Outlots 2, 3, and 6) are confirmed transferred to the Village. The developer markets a shelter, playground, and pickleball court on these outlots, but current completion has not been independently confirmed by a Village source — treat it as developer-claimed pending field or Village confirmation.
Is there pool access at Golden Ponds?
Yes, on an optional, separately paid membership basis in the Westbridge Neighborhood Pool, run by a distinct nonprofit — not automatically bundled into HOA dues. Current membership pricing was not found this research pass.
What school district serves Golden Ponds, and which elementary school?
Waunakee Community School District, confirmed via Dane County's own parcel tax-overlay records. The specific elementary school has not been confirmed — WCSD has no public address-lookup tool, and even real listings for Golden Ponds addresses list the elementary field as "Call School District." Confirm your exact address with the WCSD Registrar, (608) 849-2000, before writing an offer.
Is there a railroad near Golden Ponds?
Yes — Golden Ponds' eastern boundary adjoins an active railroad line, and the recorded declaration includes a disclosure of potential noise, vibration, exhaust, and night-operations effects, alongside a separate Right-to-Farm disclosure for nearby agricultural land.
Is Golden Ponds still building new homes, and what are homes selling for?
Yes — multiple builders (Bill Weber Jr. Homes, Coogan Builders, Classic Custom Homes of Waunakee, and others) remain active as of 2025–2026, including recent Parade of Homes participation. Sold and resale pricing requires John Reuter's SCWMLS export — contact him at 608-669-4226. A single builder-advertised new-construction listing has surfaced publicly ($924,900) but is not resale market data.
