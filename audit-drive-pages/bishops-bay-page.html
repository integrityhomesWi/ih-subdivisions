About the Community

Seven Neighborhoods,
One Master Plan

Bishops Bay — officially The Community of Bishops Bay — is not a single subdivision but a roughly 750-acre master-planned community spanning both the City of Middleton and the Town of Westport in Dane County. Its roots trace to a joint Middleton-Westport planning process, with the Middleton City Council adopting the Master Development Plan on March 1, 2011, and groundbreaking following on August 21, 2012. The plan organizes the community into seven distinct neighborhoods — Town Center, Watermark, The Back Nine, Reserve Hill, The Woods, The Farm, and The Prairie — each with its own density, lot sizes, and housing character, all built around the private Bishops Bay Country Club and its 18-hole golf course, which itself was founded in 1995.

Development has continued in stages for well over a decade. The Back Nine, the first neighborhood built, is largely built out and is now selling its final infill product, the Villas at Calloway Court. The Farm and The Prairie remain active, with current builder inventory in both. The Woods has a small number of large wooded lots still being sold, though sources reviewed for this guide describe its overall development stage inconsistently — treat it as active but lower-density rather than fully built out. Reserve Hill, originally planned around smaller, more traditional-neighborhood-style lots, was still in active redesign as of a March 2025 joint Middleton/Westport meeting, where developers proposed increasing that neighborhood from 200 to 330 lots and floated a municipal boundary change between the two jurisdictions. Watermark and the bulk of Town Center's planned commercial space remain future, unbuilt components of the plan.

Because Bishops Bay spans two municipalities and seven differently-planned neighborhoods, it should never be treated as a single, uniform subdivision — housing type, lot size, HOA obligations, and even which municipality governs a given parcel can differ from one neighborhood to the next.

"Seven neighborhoods, two municipalities, and one golf course at the center — Bishops Bay is really a collection of communities sharing a single master plan."

Know before you go: Golf club membership at The Club at Bishops Bay is entirely separate from — and optional relative to — the residential HOA. No evidence supports automatic country-club access, private Lake Mendota frontage, or individual boat slips coming with a Bishops Bay home purchase. Confirm any of these directly rather than assuming them from the community's name or general marketing — see the FAQ below.

Master Plan

Adopted by the Middleton City Council, March 1, 2011 (most recent posted revision Aug. 18, 2015). Groundbreaking August 21, 2012.

Jurisdiction

Spans the City of Middleton and the Town of Westport under a shared Joint Zoning Committee. Confirm which municipality governs a specific parcel before writing an offer — a boundary adjustment near Reserve Hill was still under discussion as of March 2025.

Scale

~750 acres · 7 planned neighborhoods · up to 2,950 residential units and 6,000+ residents at full buildout (10–20 year planning timeline).

Neighborhoods

Town Center, Watermark, The Back Nine, Reserve Hill, The Woods, The Farm, The Prairie — status and housing type vary; see Amenities and Homes sections below.

HOA

Community of Bishops Bay Home Owners Association, Inc. Current dues not publicly posted anywhere found — confirm with the community office, (608) 831-5500. Some neighborhoods carry additional sub-association fees on top of master dues.

Golf Club

The Club at Bishops Bay — private, optional, and a separate cost from HOA dues. Full Golf Memberships currently have a waitlist; official pricing is not published by the club.

Common Questions

Bishops Bay FAQ

❓ FAQ ▾
Is Bishops Bay in Middleton or Waunakee?
Neither exclusively. Bishops Bay spans the City of Middleton and the Town of Westport, two separate municipalities under a shared planning agreement. It is not in the Village of Waunakee, though some addresses in the Westport portion carry a Waunakee, WI mailing address due to standard rural postal routing.
What school district serves Bishops Bay?
Waunakee Community School District, confirmed via Dane County's own property records across both the Middleton and Westport portions. The specific elementary school assignment has not yet been confirmed to a building — ask your agent to check the district's boundary tool for a specific address, or contact the Waunakee CSD registrar directly.
Is Bishops Bay one subdivision?
No. Bishops Bay is a master-planned community of seven distinct neighborhoods — Town Center, Watermark, The Back Nine, Reserve Hill, The Woods, The Farm, and The Prairie — plus additional recorded phases and housing products, each with its own housing type, lot sizes, and development status.
Do I have to join the country club to live in Bishops Bay?
No. The Community of Bishops Bay Home Owners Association is a distinct organization from The Club at Bishops Bay Country Club. Golf and social club membership is optional, with its own separate cost and application process, currently including a waitlist for Full Golf Membership.
What does the HOA cost?
Not currently published anywhere in a primary source found during this research pass — confirm directly with the community office, (608) 831-5500, before budgeting for a home purchase. Some neighborhoods carry additional sub-association fees on top of the master dues.
Is Bishops Bay on Lake Mendota — can I have a boat or lake access?
The community is near Lake Mendota and the golf course has lake views, but no evidence was found of private lakefront lots or individually owned boat slips/docks. The country club offers a reservable club pontoon boat as a membership perk. Confirm any lake-access expectations directly rather than assuming them from the community's name or general marketing.
Is Bishops Bay still under construction?
Yes, in parts. Some neighborhoods, like The Back Nine, are largely built out, while others — Reserve Hill and Watermark — are still in planning or unbuilt as of this research date, and at least one neighborhood's development plan and municipal boundary were still being actively negotiated as of March 2025.
What are homes selling for in Bishops Bay?
Contact John Reuter at 608-669-4226 for current SCWMLS subdivision-specific sold data and a market analysis — this figure should never be sourced from Zillow, Redfin, or similar third-party estimate sites. Current builder-quoted new-construction pricing ranges from roughly the upper $700s to $2 million-plus depending on builder, lot, and neighborhood, but builder-advertised prices are not resale market data.

Common Questions

Bishops Bay FAQ

❓ FAQ ▾
Is Bishops Bay in Middleton or Waunakee?
Neither exclusively. Bishops Bay spans the City of Middleton and the Town of Westport, two separate municipalities under a shared planning agreement. It is not in the Village of Waunakee, though some addresses in the Westport portion carry a Waunakee, WI mailing address due to standard rural postal routing.
What school district serves Bishops Bay?
Waunakee Community School District, confirmed via Dane County's own property records across both the Middleton and Westport portions. The specific elementary school assignment has not yet been confirmed to a building — ask your agent to check the district's boundary tool for a specific address, or contact the Waunakee CSD registrar directly.
Is Bishops Bay one subdivision?
No. Bishops Bay is a master-planned community of seven distinct neighborhoods — Town Center, Watermark, The Back Nine, Reserve Hill, The Woods, The Farm, and The Prairie — plus additional recorded phases and housing products, each with its own housing type, lot sizes, and development status.
Do I have to join the country club to live in Bishops Bay?
No. The Community of Bishops Bay Home Owners Association is a distinct organization from The Club at Bishops Bay Country Club. Golf and social club membership is optional, with its own separate cost and application process, currently including a waitlist for Full Golf Membership.
What does the HOA cost?
Not currently published anywhere in a primary source found during this research pass — confirm directly with the community office, (608) 831-5500, before budgeting for a home purchase. Some neighborhoods carry additional sub-association fees on top of the master dues.
Is Bishops Bay on Lake Mendota — can I have a boat or lake access?
The community is near Lake Mendota and the golf course has lake views, but no evidence was found of private lakefront lots or individually owned boat slips/docks. The country club offers a reservable club pontoon boat as a membership perk. Confirm any lake-access expectations directly rather than assuming them from the community's name or general marketing.
Is Bishops Bay still under construction?
Yes, in parts. Some neighborhoods, like The Back Nine, are largely built out, while others — Reserve Hill and Watermark — are still in planning or unbuilt as of this research date, and at least one neighborhood's development plan and municipal boundary were still being actively negotiated as of March 2025.

Current builder-quoted new-construction pricing ranges from roughly the upper $700s to $2 million-plus depending on builder, lot, and neighborhood, but builder-advertised prices are not resale market data.
