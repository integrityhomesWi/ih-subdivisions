# Integrity Homes — Transaction Email Templates

A library of 10 reusable emails John Reuter sends during real estate transactions. When John asks for any of these, reproduce the matching template, fill in the `[bracketed]` fields from the details he gives, and ask only for the fields you can't infer. **Preserve the wording, structure, and sign-off exactly** — these are John's approved templates. Always end with the standard sign-off:

```
John Reuter
Integrity Homes  |  Reward Our Heroes™
john@integrityhomeswi.com
```

## How to choose the right template

| # | Template | When to use | TO / CC |
|---|----------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | Seller Information Questionnaire | After meeting a new seller, before listing | New seller |
| 2 | New File Request | Right after offer accepted — open title file (attach signed contract) | Title company; CC closing officer, Michele |
| 3 | Accepted Offer (Co-op Agent / Lender) | Notify other agent/lender, confirm deadlines | Co-op agent or lender; CC Michele |
| 4 | Accepted Offer – Intake / Handoff (Internal) | Hand transaction to team admin | Team admin (Lindsay / Michele) |
| 5 | You're Officially Under Contract! (Buyer) | Congratulate buyer, lay out summary + next steps | Buyer; CC Michele, lender |
| 6 | You're Under Contract (Seller) | Notify seller, set title-company expectations | Seller |
| 7 | New Transaction Summary (Records) | Full recap for client records or file | Client or self |
| 8 | Closing Day is Tomorrow! | Day before closing — funds + ID reminder | Client (buyer or seller) |
| 9 | Congratulations on Your New Home! (Post-Closing) | After closing — warm thank-you | Buyer |
| 10 | Offer to Purchase (OTP) Cover Email | Submitting buyer's offer to listing agent (attach OTP + pre-qual) | Listing agent |

## 1. Seller Information Questionnaire
**WHEN TO USE** After meeting with a new seller, before listing — to collect details upfront.
**TO** New seller (client)

**Subject:** Seller Information Questionnaire

Hi [Seller Name],

Thank you again for taking the time to talk with me about selling your home. I'm looking forward to working with you.

As a next step, I've included a short Seller Information Questionnaire below. Prior to listing your home, we like to gather as much information as possible upfront to ensure the process is smooth, accurate, and as stress-free as possible. This helps us prepare your listing, disclosures, marketing, and showings efficiently while minimizing surprises later.

Please complete the form here: https://forms.gle/bWosY9jTfKY6GqcAA

Most sellers are able to complete this in about 10–15 minutes. If you're unsure about any questions, approximations are perfectly fine, and you can always leave something blank and we'll review it together.

Once I receive your responses, I'll take care of the next steps and follow up with you if anything needs clarification.

As always, if you have questions at any point, don't hesitate to reach out. One question that is not on the form is if you would like a yard sign or not. Please let me know — it would be included as part of our fee.

Talk soon and look forward to helping you.

John Reuter
Integrity Homes  |  Reward Our Heroes™
john@integrityhomeswi.com

## 6. You're Under Contract (to Seller)
**WHEN TO USE** Notify the seller that their home is under contract and set expectations on the title company contact.
**TO** Seller (client)

**Subject:** You're Under Contract - [Property Address]

Hi [Seller Name(s)],

Great news - your home at [Property Address] is officially under contract!

I'll keep you informed at every milestone and ensure everything stays on track from here to closing.

The title company will reach out to you to obtain important information such as social security numbers, loan numbers, and date of birth. If you want to verify their identity before providing that information, please feel free to call or text Michele. We want to ensure you feel comfortable.

We will let you know once the buyers schedule the home inspection.

Congratulations again on your accepted offer!

John Reuter
