## Funnel Awareness

Assess where the topic sits before writing:

- **TOFU (awareness):** Educate and build trust. Soft CTA — "follow for more" or "bookmark this."
- **MOFU (consideration):** Demonstrate expertise, address specific concerns. Medium CTA — "download the guide" or "schedule a free consultation."
- **BOFU (decision):** Specificity, local authority, direct CTAs — "call or text me at 608-669-4226."

Identify the stage and adjust tone, specificity, and CTA strength accordingly.

## Blog Structure (AI-Search Optimized)

### 1. Snippet Answer (Top of Post)
Restate the core question in **bold**, followed by a 1-2 sentence answer (under 250 characters). Include the market area and primary keyword. This is what AI search engines pull as a direct answer.

### 2. Introduction (2-3 paragraphs)
Hook the reader with a surprising stat, relatable scenario, or direct statement of what they'll learn. No throat-clearing or generic "current market" openers.

### 3. Body (H2/H3 subheadings)
- Short paragraphs: 2-4 sentences max
- Use bullets and tables only when they genuinely help (data comparisons, feature lists) — not as default formatting
- Every section advances understanding or answers a follow-up question
- Keywords naturally incorporated — Integrity Homes, location terms, neighborhood names, real estate terms
- Location mentioned at least 3 times with variations (city, neighborhood, ZIP, county)

### 4. FAQ Section
2-3 Q&As at the bottom reflecting likely "People Also Ask" queries. These feed AI search engines directly.

### 5. ROH Footer Block
Close every IH blog with the Reward Our Heroes brand block. This is boilerplate — it is NOT ROH content and does not trigger the ROH Content Writer skill.

### 6. CTA
One clear CTA matching the funnel stage. Default: "Call or text me at 608-669-4226."

## Constraints

- **No fabricated data.** If John hasn't provided market stats, write from general knowledge or ask. Never invent Dane County numbers.
- **Fair Housing compliance.** No school quality ratings, no demographic references, no language that implies neighborhood composition.
- **No em-dashes** anywhere in the post.
- **Integrity Homes** is the brand name in all visible copy. "Integrity Homes of Wisconsin" = legal name, JSON-LD only. "Integrity Homes Wisconsin" (no "of") is forbidden.
